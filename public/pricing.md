## Credits find your leads. Everything else is included.

A search uses credits. So does a work email. Nothing else does.

- A search: the cost varies, and you see it first. Pancake finds people and checks each one against your ICP. A narrow audience takes more research. Credits a search doesn't use come back.
- A work email: 50 credits. Charged only when Pancake finds one. No email, no credits.

What never uses a credit:

- Building and testing your Plays
- Your Brain, built from your website
- A warm-up, then up to three messages
- Replies stop the sequence and get sorted
- New leads in Slack and an 08:30 email
- Pancake in Claude and Codex

## No surprise bills

- You see the cost first. Pancake shows an estimate before every search.
- A slow month isn't lost. Unused credits roll over. Your balance holds up to two months of them.
- Never billed beyond your plan. Run out, and searches pause. No overage, no automatic top-up.
- More credits cost less. Up to 18% less per credit at the higher levels.

## One plan per workspace

A workspace is one company. Its plan covers the Brain, every Play and your whole team. They share one balance of credits.

- Brain: what Pancake knows about your company and how you sell. Every Play uses it.
- Play: its own audience and messages. As many as you want.
- Team: Invite your whole team at no extra cost.
- Another company? That's a second workspace, with its own Brain, its own plan and its own credits.

## Questions

### I sell to two audiences. One workspace or two?

One. Give each audience its own Play, with its own targeting and messages. Both Plays share the Brain and the credits.

### Can I send from more than one account?

Yes. One sending account is included. Each extra account is $39 a month. A Play sends from one account or rotates across up to ten.

### Do I pay per teammate?

No. Invite your whole team to the workspace.

### When do I need a second workspace?

When you run a second company or brand. It gets its own Brain, plan and credits, charged to the same card from day one, without a second trial.

## Billing questions

### What do credits pay for?

Two things: the research a search does to find and check your leads, and each work email found, at 50 credits. Building and testing Plays is free.

### How many leads do I get for my credits?

It depends on who you look for: a narrow audience takes more research per lead. Pancake shows an estimate before every search, and the trial lets you measure it on your own audience.

### Do unused credits roll over?

Yes. At each renewal you keep what is left, up to one month of credits. On 60,000 credits a month, your balance holds up to 120,000.

### What happens when I run out of credits?

Searches pause, and your Brain, Plays and leads stay. You are never billed extra. Move up a level and keep what is left, or start your next month early.

### Can I change my monthly credits?

Yes. More credits start today, and you keep the ones you have. Fewer credits start at your next renewal.

### How does the free trial work?

Sign up free. Pancake reads your website, builds your Brain and shows the first leads it found. Add a card to start the trial: 7 days and 15,000 credits.

### What happens when the trial ends?

Your plan starts on the card you added, with the monthly credits you picked. Unused trial credits end with the trial. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no long-term commitment. You keep access and your credits to the end of the billing period you paid for.

### Do I need my own data tools or API keys?

No. Your credits pay for the lead data and the ICP check. Claude, Claude Code and Codex connect with a browser sign-in, no API key.

### Can I use Pancake from my AI agent?

Yes, on the same plan. Claude, Claude Code and Codex use the same Brain, Plays, leads and credits as the app.

### Have a code?

Enter it when you add your card. It applies to your monthly payments after the trial.
