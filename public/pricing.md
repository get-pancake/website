# Pancake pricing: $99 a month per workspace

> One plan, everything included. No hidden fees, no surprises. 3-day free trial. Credit card required to start the trial.

This file carries the facts of https://pancake.ai/pricing for AI agents. The page is the source of truth.

- Start free: https://app.pancake.ai
- Book a demo: https://pancake.ai/demo

## The plan

- Price: $99 a month per workspace.
- Your whole team included.
- As many Plays and leads as you want.
- Warm leads every morning.
- A personal message for each lead, in your voice.
- You approve every lead. You stay in control.
- Free trial: 3 days. Signup starts without a card. A credit card is required to start the trial.

## One plan per workspace

A workspace is one company. Its plan covers the Brain, every Play, your whole team and the accounts you send from.

- Brain: what Pancake knows about your company and how you sell. Every Play uses it.
- Play: its own audience and messages. As many as you want.
- Team: Invite your whole team at no extra cost.
- Another company? That's a second workspace, with its own Brain and its own $99 plan.

## Everything in the $99

- Your Brain, built from your website
- A fresh search every night, on your schedule
- An ICP check and a reason on every lead
- A warm-up, then up to three messages
- Replies stop the sequence and get sorted
- New leads in Slack and an 08:30 email
- Pancake in Claude and Codex

## Questions

### I sell to two audiences. One workspace or two?

One. Give each audience its own Play, with its own targeting, messages and accounts. Both Plays share the Brain.

### Can I send from more than one account?

Yes, at no extra cost. Each Play sends from one account or rotates across up to ten.

### Do I pay per teammate?

No. Invite your whole team to the workspace.

### When do I need a second workspace?

When you run a second company or brand. It gets its own Brain and its own $99 plan, charged to the same card from day one, without a second trial.

## Billing questions

### How does the free trial work?

Sign up free. Pancake reads your website, builds your Brain and shows the first leads it found. Add a card to start the 3-day free trial.

### What happens when the trial ends?

Your plan starts at $99 a month per workspace, on the card you added. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no long-term commitment. You keep access to the end of the billing period you paid for.

### Do I need my own data tools or API keys?

No. Lead data and the ICP check are in the plan. Claude, Claude Code and Codex connect with a browser sign-in, no API key.

### Is there a limit on leads?

Each Play search keeps up to 50 new leads, and every Play searches again each night.

### Can I use Pancake from my AI agent?

Yes, on the same plan. Claude, Claude Code and Codex use the same Brain, Plays and leads as the app.

### Have a code?

Enter it when you add your card. It applies to your monthly payments after the trial.
