## What goes into one lead

Leads matched to your business, not to a filter. Your GTM Brain, 30+ data providers and outreach in your voice work on every lead. All in the plan.

- Your GTM Brain: Pancake reads your website and learns your ICP, your personas, your competitors and your voice. Every search and message starts from that context, and it learns from each lead you approve or pass.
- 30+ data providers, included: Dealroom, TheirStack, Coresignal and more, in one plan. No contract to sign, no API key, no data bill on the side.
- Live buying signals: funding rounds, open roles, tech stacks, posts and who reacts to them. A Play keeps searching on your schedule, so new leads keep coming.
- Outreach from your account: a personal message for each lead, in your voice, sent from your own account once you approve the lead. A reply stops the sequence.

## Billing questions

### How many leads will I get?

About 120 to 600 qualified leads a month on 60,000 credits, and twice that at each level up. Each one is matched to your business by your GTM Brain, on data from 30+ providers, and gets a personal message. It is an estimate: a narrow audience costs more, and Pancake shows the real one before a search runs.

### How does the free trial work?

Sign up free. Pancake reads your website, builds your Brain and shows the first leads it found. Add a card to start the trial: 7 days and 15,000 credits, about 30 to 150 leads.

### What happens when the trial ends?

Your plan starts on the card you added, on the level you picked. Cancel before the trial ends and you pay nothing.

### Can I start on a higher level, or change later?

Yes. Pick your level at the top of the pricing page and Start free opens Pancake on it. Later, more credits start the same day and you keep the ones you have. Fewer credits start at your next renewal.

### What happens when I run out of credits?

Searches pause. Your leads, messages and replies stay, and you are never billed extra. Move up a level, or start your next month early.

### Do unused credits roll over?

Yes. At each renewal you keep what is left, up to one month of credits.

### Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There is no long-term commitment. You keep access and your credits to the end of the period you paid for.

### Do I pay per teammate?

No. Invite your whole team to the workspace.

### Can I send from more than one account?

Yes. One sending account is included. Each extra account is $39 a month. A Play sends from one account or rotates across up to ten.

### I sell to two audiences. Do I need two plans?

No. Give each audience its own Play, with its own targeting and messages, on the same plan. A second company or brand is a second workspace, with its own plan.

### Do I need my own data tools or API keys?

No. Your credits pay for the lead data and the ICP check. Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT and Codex connect with a browser sign-in, no API key.

### Can I use Pancake from Claude or Codex?

Yes, on the same plan. Claude, Claude Code, ChatGPT and Codex use the same Plays, leads and credits as the app.

### Have a code?

Enter it when you add your card. It applies to your monthly payments after the trial.
