## What Gojiberry does well

Gojiberry is backed by Y Combinator and sells an AI agent that "finds high intent leads and contacts them for you." You enter your website. It learns what you sell and who buys it, then goes looking for them.

Its signal list is broad. Gojiberry watches people who engage with your company or content, visit your profile, follow your company page, engage around competitors or talk about relevant topics. It also tracks job changes, hiring activity and lookalikes of your best customers. Each lead is enriched, checked against your ideal customer profile and scored before outreach starts.

Outreach runs across email and the professional network with AI-written messages and automatic follow-ups, and there are no sequences to build. You choose full autopilot or approve each message before it sends. Replies land in a unified inbox with an answer already drafted. The agent tracks what converts and adjusts week by week. Pro also connects to HubSpot, Pipedrive and Claude.

At $99 a month, that is a lot of outbound in one tool. Our [Gojiberry vs Pancake comparison](/gojiberry-vs-pancake) lays out where each one fits.

## Why people look for Gojiberry alternatives

The reason to switch depends on the team. Five come up most.

- **Volume and team size.** Pro covers two agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted a month. Teams of five or more and agencies move to a Custom plan through a demo.
- **Channel mix.** Some founders want every conversation to open warm on the network where their buyers already post. Larger sales teams want the opposite: more channels, a contact database and a phone dialer.
- **Outbound is one motion.** Gojiberry is built to run outreach. Teams that also want buyers to find them in Google and in AI answers need content too.
- **Research depth.** Some buyers judge a tool on how much research sits behind each message, and shop on that alone.
- **Budget.** Teams that already know their targets and write their own sequences can pay less for plain automation.

## 1. Pancake: for buyers you reach and buyers who find you

Pancake is an AI GTM team for founders and small B2B companies. Like Gojiberry, it starts from your website and costs $99 a month. From there, its agents pick people by the buying signal they show, open the conversation from your own account, and write articles that help buyers find you in search.

- **Turns your site into a GTM Brain.** Your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections sit in one shared memory. Each result teaches it more.
- **Tracks six signal types.** Someone posts about a topic you name. Someone engages with a competitor, with a voice your buyers follow, or with your own posts. A company opens a role that fits your buyer, or posts a job that names a tool you replace.
- **Hands you new leads each morning.** Every lead carries its reason, the signal that surfaced it, so you know why this person and why now. New leads land in Slack as well, and you can approve them there without opening the app.
- **Opens warm, from your own account.** A profile visit, a like on a recent post, an invite with no note, then up to three messages. Message one makes no pitch. It asks a single light question about the signal.
- **Brings buyers in through search.** Pancake drafts articles built to show up in Google and in AI answers. Articles go through your approval, like leads do.
- **Runs from Claude, ChatGPT or Codex.** Through Pancake's MCP server, your assistant reads the GTM Brain, checks leads and starts outreach in the chat you already use.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month, flat](/pricing). Every agent is in it, with no seats, no tiers and no usage billing. You need a card to start the 3-day free trial.

**How it differs from Gojiberry:** Gojiberry puts its $99 into outbound volume across two channels. Pancake puts it into two motions: warm conversations that open on a question about the signal, and articles that bring buyers to you. Pancake keeps outreach on the professional network, so a team that wants email follow-ups in every sequence will find them in Gojiberry, lemlist or Amplemarket.

**Pick it if** you run a founder-led or small B2B team, you want every conversation to start from a reason, and you want search working for you while outreach runs. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. Valley: the closest like-for-like swap

Valley calls itself the complete AI outbound system. It finds people showing interest: profile viewers, post engagers, company followers and people engaging with competitors. It researches each lead across more than 200 sources, including blogs, newsletters, videos and press, then writes a message in your voice. Invites and messages go out on the professional network, emails go out from your own inbox, and Valley handles follow-ups and replies. You approve each message or let Valley send on autopilot. Valley Deep lets you describe a buyer in plain English and find them across the live web.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Everything plan costs $149 a month billed quarterly or $199 month to month, for one seat. Everything + Deep costs $419 a month billed quarterly or $499 monthly and adds $300 a month of Deep credits. Scale pricing for five or more seats goes through sales. The Everything plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

**How it differs from Gojiberry:** the two are built on the same idea. Both start from signals and write personal messages across email and the professional network. Valley puts more weight on the research behind each message, and it costs more per seat.

**Pick it if** you like Gojiberry's model and want more depth in every message.

## 3. Amplemarket: a full sales platform with AI on top

Amplemarket sells an all-in-one AI sales platform. Its agent, Duo, captures signals such as a decision maker visiting your website or profile, someone following your company or commenting on your posts, a review that complains about a competitor, or a contact changing jobs. Duo then builds sequences with email, call and social steps. Higher plans add AI voice notes and drafted replies. Around it sit a B2B contact database, a parallel dialer, deliverability tools and, on every plan, MCP access for Claude and ChatGPT.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Startup costs $600 a month on an annual term, with two users and 27,000 contacts. Growth and Elite are custom-priced. All plans include a free trial.

**How it differs from Gojiberry:** Amplemarket is a sales team's whole stack, with data, phone and email under one contract. It costs several times more and asks for a year's commitment.

**Pick it if** you run a sales team and want the database, dialer, sequences and signals in one place.

## 4. Origami: open-web research, paid by credits

Origami promises to get you customers. You enter your website and it recommends plays, such as companies hiring salespeople or sales leaders new in their role. You approve a play with a click and Origami runs the campaign. Each search runs across more than 50 real-time sources, including Google Maps, social profiles, job boards, company sites and the open web. Paid plans include its Sequencer: email and social cadences of up to 10 steps, with one shared inbox for replies. Pro adds a content mode that drafts posts.

**Pricing (September 2026):** a free plan with 1,000 one-time credits and no card, capped at 30 rows per table. Starter costs $29 a month with 2,000 credits, 5 email senders and 1 social sender. Pro costs $129 with 10,000 credits, and Max $399 with 30,000. Credits pay for data such as new leads, verified emails and enrichments. Every paid plan includes unlimited team members.

**How it differs from Gojiberry:** Origami searches wider than social activity, into local businesses, hiring and tech stacks. Cost follows how much data you pull. Our [Origami vs Pancake page](/origami-vs-pancake) covers the research-versus-conversations split.

**Pick it if** your buyers are spread across the open web, or you want to build lists before you decide how to reach them.

## 5. lemlist: multichannel campaigns you control

Sales teams use lemlist to run outbound across email, the professional network, calls, SMS and WhatsApp. It comes with a lead database of more than 650 million contacts, an email and phone finder, a deliverability hub, and lemAgent, a set of AI agents for signals, enrichment and messaging. It also ships an MCP server and a CLI.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Email plan costs $69 a month, or $55 billed yearly, with unlimited users and 50,000 emails a month. Multichannel costs $109 per user a month, or $87 billed yearly, and adds social steps, SMS and a built-in dialer. Buying intent signals are a data add-on at $29 a month ($21 billed yearly) for 100 signals. The 14-day free trial needs no card.

**How it differs from Gojiberry:** you design each campaign, and lemlist gives you control over every step. Gojiberry decides who to contact and what to send. Buying signals come as a separate add-on. See [lemlist vs Pancake](/lemlist-vs-pancake) for the founder-side view.

**Pick it if** someone on your team likes building campaigns and email volume is central to your plan.

## 6. Dripify: plain sequence automation on a budget

Dripify automates outreach sequences on the professional network and by email. A drag-and-drop builder offers more than 10 actions and conditions. An AI icebreaker variable personalizes the opening line. Dripify lists nine lead sources, including the people engaging with any post you pick. Intent signals are marked as coming soon. Pro adds a dedicated inbox, CSV export, webhooks and HubSpot. Each seat connects one account.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Basic costs $39 per user a month billed annually ($59 monthly), Pro $59 ($79) and Advanced $79 ($99). Enterprise is custom. Every plan includes 100 email finder credits a month. The 7-day free trial needs no card.

**How it differs from Gojiberry:** Dripify runs the sequences you design. You pick the sources and write the messages. Gojiberry does both for you.

**Pick it if** you know exactly who to target, write your own messages, and want a low price per seat.
