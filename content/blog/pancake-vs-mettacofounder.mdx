Pancake and mettaCofounder both help independent experts win clients. They go about it in opposite ways.

mettaCofounder is a small Lisbon team that runs marketing and sales alongside you for six months. It works with executive coaches, consultants and fractional leaders. Pancake is an AI GTM team. Its agents find people who show buying signals, run outreach from your own account and write articles built to show up in Google and AI answers.

**TL;DR:** Pick mettaCofounder if you need people to shape your positioning, write your content and coach you through sales calls, and you can commit €8,999 for six months. Pick Pancake if you already know who buys from you and need a steady flow of conversations with them, for $99/month flat. A consultant can run both: mettaCofounder for the offer, Pancake for the pipeline.

## What mettaCofounder does

mettaCofounder sells marketing and sales done with you. Its founders, Carsten Pfisterer and Olga Kirillova, spent years at Adidas and Wayfair before starting MettaStartup Studio. The team is based in Lisbon, Portugal, and says it has supported more than 100 founders.

It is built for one kind of business: an expert who sells their own knowledge. The site names executive coaches, business consultants and fractional leaders, plus solo founders and knowledge entrepreneurs.

**Core use case:** You left a VP role six months ago to work as a fractional CFO. You have the expertise. You don't have a sharp niche, a content habit or a sales process. mettaCofounder builds those with you.

### The Rent a Cofounder partnership

The main offer is a six-month engagement. As of September 2026, the team handles:

1. **Customer discovery:** five interviews with your prospects, run by the mettaCofounder team.
2. **Positioning and offer:** who you serve, what you sell and how you talk about it.
3. **Content:** a content strategy and three posts a week written for you, drawn from stories you share on a monthly intake call.
4. **Funnel:** sales pages, email sequences and automation for bookings, payments and your CRM pipeline.
5. **Sales coaching:** sessions on how to run the client conversation.

You get a dedicated account manager, a content manager and monthly strategy reviews with the two founders. Your part is the onboarding calls, the weekly strategy calls and the sales conversations themselves.

The tool setup includes a Claude project with custom content and outreach skills, a CRM with pipeline automation, and a cold outreach system. At month six the team hands everything over. You can renew, move to the AI Cofounder subscription (an AI trained on your voice, with a community and the full system) or run it on your own.

**Where it stops:** every program is shaped around experts who sell their own time. The work centers on positioning, a personal brand and sales conversations, delivered by people with AI tools in support.

## What Pancake does

Pancake does the prospecting a busy expert never gets to. Its agents spot people showing buying signals, bring you new leads every morning and start the conversation from your account. It is built for founders and small B2B companies.

You start by adding your website. Pancake learns who buys from you (positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof, objections) and keeps it in one shared memory called the GTM Brain. That memory keeps learning from what works.

Each morning, new leads arrive. Every lead shows the signal that picked it: a post about a topic you chose, a like on a competitor's post, a job opening that matches your buyer. You approve leads in the app or from Slack. For each approved lead, Pancake runs outreach from your own account on the professional social network: a profile visit, a like on a recent post, an invite with no note, then up to three messages. The first message asks one light question tied to the signal and makes no pitch.

Pancake also writes articles for Google and AI search, and you approve each one. If you already work in Claude, ChatGPT or Codex, its MCP server lets you review leads and start outreach from the same conversation. It costs [$99/month flat](/pricing), every agent included, with a 3-day free trial (a credit card is required to start it).

## When you'd use Pancake

**Use Pancake when:**
- You sell to other businesses and can already say who your buyer is
- Your offer is clear, and the gap is too few conversations with the right people
- You want each first message to start from a reason the prospect will recognize
- You want search articles written for you while the outreach runs
- You'd rather pay month to month than commit to a six-month engagement

Consultants with a set offer are a strong fit. [Pancake for consultants](/for/consultants) shows what the signals look like for an advisory practice.

## When you'd use mettaCofounder (and when you wouldn't)

**Use mettaCofounder when:**
- You left a senior corporate role (VP, Director, Principal) in the last few years and launched a solo consulting or coaching practice
- You have deep domain expertise (finance, ops, HR, supply chain, digital transformation) but little marketing or sales muscle
- You want people to write your content, build your pages and coach your sales conversations
- You can commit six months and a four-figure euro budget
- You want the whole system handed over at the end so you can run it yourself

**Don't use mettaCofounder if:**
- You're running a product company (SaaS, e-commerce, marketplace). mettaCofounder's clients are coaches, consultants and fractional leaders.
- You already have a repeatable way to win clients. You'd be paying to rebuild what works.
- You need a monthly plan you can stop at any time. The main offer is a six-month partnership.

**Typical fits:**
- A former finance director now selling fractional CFO services, who needs a niche and a steady content habit
- An ex-brand lead launching a strategy consultancy, who wants the offer, pages and outreach built in one go
- An independent executive coach who has the expertise and wants a sales process for the first ten clients

## What "cofounder" means here

mettaCofounder puts the word in its name. Its main offer, Rent a Cofounder, is a team that works next to you for six months the way a marketing and sales partner would. The AI Cofounder subscription carries the system on after the handover.

Pancake is not a co-founder, human or AI. It is an AI GTM team built for one job, customers: its agents find buyers, open conversations with them and publish articles for Google and AI search.

There is little overlap between the two. The one place they meet is outreach: mettaCofounder sets up a cold outreach system for you to run, while Pancake runs its own outreach from your account.
