## What Artisan does well

Artisan puts the whole outbound stack in one product. Ava searches a database of more than 250 million verified B2B contacts, enriched from dozens of sources. She watches intent signals such as funding rounds, leadership hires and website visits, and launches outreach when one fires. Then she writes personalized sequences across email and social, handles replies and objections, and books meetings on your reps' calendars.

It handles the sending plumbing too. Artisan buys your domains and mailboxes, warms them up and monitors deliverability. Ava sends in your reps' names from your own sending domains. She doesn't place calls herself: she queues leads in a built-in power dialer, with talking points, for your reps. She also tests message variations and shifts volume toward the ones that get replies.

You keep the controls. In approval mode, outreach waits for your sign-off before it goes out. You can lock tone and calls to action, ban phrases and set escalation rules. Artisan holds SOC 2 Type II certification and syncs both ways with HubSpot, and with Salesforce on its sales-led plans.

Starting is low-risk. The Free plan covers lead search and enrichment. New accounts get a 30-day trial with 10,000 credits, and no card is needed.

For a sales team with reps who want more meetings on the calendar, Artisan is a strong pick.

## Why people look for Artisan alternatives

Teams that leave Artisan tend to name one of four reasons.

- **Credit math.** Artisan estimates a full campaign at about 20 credits per person contacted. The Intern plan's 10,000 monthly credits cover about 500 people. Mailboxes and phone numbers are billed on top, and a power dialer seat costs $75 a month ($67 billed annually).
- **An email-first motion.** Ava's core channel is cold email from dedicated sending domains and mailboxes, with social steps alongside. Teams that would rather not add sending domains, or whose buyers answer on the professional network, look elsewhere.
- **Built around reps.** Ava books meetings for reps, queues calls for reps and sends as reps. The Team and Scale plans are priced through a sales call. A founder who sells alone may want a setup shaped for founder-led sales.
- **Where the list starts.** Artisan starts from a large database and triggers on company events such as funding and hiring. Some teams would rather start from people already engaging: posting about the problem, reacting to a competitor, or commenting on their own posts.

## 1. Pancake: the pick for founders and small teams who want buyers

Pancake is an AI GTM team built for founders and small B2B companies. Its AI agents find the people showing buying signals and start the conversations for you. Where Ava starts from a contact database, Pancake starts from what your buyers are doing this week.

- **Learns your market.** Add your website and Pancake builds its GTM Brain: your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. Each result makes it sharper.
- **Watches six kinds of signals.** On the people side: posts about a topic you choose, and engagement with a competitor, with a voice your buyers follow, or with your own posts. On the company side: open roles that match your buyer, and job posts that name a tool you replace.
- **Brings leads every morning.** Each one carries the reason it was picked. It lands in Slack too, so you can approve it there or in the app.
- **Opens warm, from your own account.** On the professional network, Pancake visits the profile, likes a recent post and sends an invite with no note, then up to three messages. No pitch in the first one: it asks a light question about the signal that surfaced the person.
- **Writes for search and AI answers.** Pancake drafts articles written to show up in Google results and AI answers. You approve each one.
- **Runs from Claude or ChatGPT.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Codex to Pancake's MCP server, and you can review leads, tune signals and launch outreach without leaving the chat.

**Pricing (September 2026):** one plan at [$99 a month, flat](/pricing), with every agent in it. No seats and no credits. A card is needed to start the 3-day free trial.

**How it differs from Artisan:** Ava fills your reps' calendars from a database, at volume. Pancake starts conversations with people already showing a buying signal, from your own account, and writes articles for Google and AI search. Artisan's bill moves with credits, mailboxes and dialer seats. Pancake's price is the same every month.

**Pick it if** you are a founder or a small B2B team without a sales team, and you want more conversations with the right buyers. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. 11x: the enterprise AI SDR

11x calls its agents digital workers. Alice is its outbound AI SDR. She finds buyers across 50+ data sources, researches each one and writes a personal message. Her sequences run across email, phone, chat, social networks, SMS and WhatsApp, and she handles replies and drives qualified meetings. Julian, its AI phone agent, covers inbound: speed to lead, qualification and scheduling. 11x also tracks website visitors, writes outreach in several languages and is SOC 2 compliant.

**Pricing:** not public. 11x sells through a live demo, and its site has no pricing page.

**How it differs from Artisan:** 11x covers more channels, including SMS, WhatsApp and AI voice, and pairs outbound with an inbound phone agent. Artisan publishes self-serve prices and a free plan. 11x offers neither.

**Pick it if** you run a larger sales org and want one vendor for outbound and inbound AI work.

## 3. AiSDR: the self-serve AI SDR

AiSDR runs AI-researched outreach by email and on the professional network, with call steps through Aircall. Its GTM agent, Ami, reads your website, drafts a strategy and builds campaigns. AiSDR sets up and warms your mailboxes and syncs both ways with HubSpot. It answers replies in 5 to 10 minutes, or drafts them for you in co-pilot mode. The Explore plan adds social signals: post engagement, profile visits and keyword tracking.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Solo costs $250 a month, month to month, for 200 AI-researched contacts, one user, three mailboxes and one social account. Explore costs $900 a month for 800 contacts. Scale costs $2,500 a month for 2,500 contacts. Explore and Scale sit on quarterly contracts.

**How it differs from Artisan:** AiSDR prices by researched contacts instead of credits, and it publishes every tier. Solo costs the same $250 as Artisan's Intern plan billed annually, but runs month to month. Artisan's Intern reaches more people for that money, about 500 a month.

**Pick it if** you want an AI SDR you can switch on this week and pay for month to month.

## 4. Amplemarket: AI inside a full sales platform

Amplemarket is an all-in-one sales platform with an AI layer called Duo. Duo watches for signals such as visits to your website or profile, competitor reviews and job changes. It learns your company from your website, the web and your CRM. Then it drafts a sequence with email, call and social steps for each lead, and a rep decides whether to send it. The platform bundles a B2B database, a dialer, deliverability tools and an MCP add-on for working from Claude or ChatGPT.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Startup runs $600 a month on an annual term and includes two users and 27,000 contacts. Growth and Elite are priced on request, and each plan comes with a free trial.

**How it differs from Artisan:** Amplemarket keeps your reps in charge, with Duo helping at each step. Artisan hands the motion to Ava. Amplemarket also sells on an annual term with users included, where Artisan's Intern plan can run monthly.

**Pick it if** you have reps who will work in the tool every day and want AI to do the research and drafting.

## 5. Valley: warm outreach on the professional network

Valley starts from people who already showed interest: your profile viewers, post engagers and followers, plus people engaging with competitors. It scores each lead against your ideal customer profile and drops the poor fits. Valley says it researches 200+ data points per prospect, then writes the message in your voice. You approve each message or let it send within the network's safety limits. Email is available too.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Everything costs $199 a month month to month, or $149 a month billed quarterly. Everything + Deep costs $499 a month ($419 billed quarterly) and adds $300 a month in credits to find buyers across the live web. A done-for-you service costs $1,499 a month. Everything has a 7-day free trial. Valley's plan table lists your own profile viewers, followers and post engagers as a $50 a month add-on on that plan.

**How it differs from Artisan:** Valley is social-first and starts from warm signals. Artisan is email-first and starts from its database. Valley's entry plan also costs less than Artisan's Intern plan.

**Pick it if** your buyers are active on the professional network and you want to run warm social outreach yourself.

## 6. Apollo: the budget pick with a free plan

Apollo pairs a database of 240 million contacts and 30 million companies with sequences, a dialer, deliverability tools and AI research. Its Professional plan adds agents that work in the background, and Apollo offers an MCP connection too. You build the lists and the sequences. The AI helps with research and lead scoring.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Free, with 900 credits per seat per year and two sequences. Basic costs $49 per seat per month billed annually, Professional $79, and Organization $119 with a three-seat minimum. You can trial Basic or Professional for 14 days.

**How it differs from Artisan:** Apollo is a toolkit your team drives. Ava runs the campaign for you. Apollo costs far less to start and bills per seat.

**Pick it if** budget is tight and someone on your team will build and run the outbound.

## Which Artisan alternative should you choose?

Match the tool to the job you hired Ava for.

- **"Find our buyers and start conversations with them."** Pancake.
- **"An AI SDR on every channel, plus an inbound phone agent."** 11x.
- **"An AI SDR we can start this week, month to month."** AiSDR.
- **"AI help for our reps inside one sales platform."** Amplemarket.
- **"Warm social outreach we run ourselves."** Valley.
- **"Outbound on a tight budget."** Apollo.

Pancake runs its outreach on the professional network, from your own account. If high-volume email is your main channel, Artisan, AiSDR and Apollo are built for that job.

And if Ava keeps your reps' calendars full at a credit spend that works for you, stay on Artisan. A switch would cost more than it saves.
