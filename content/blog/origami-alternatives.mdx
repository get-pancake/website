## What Origami does well

Origami's pitch is short: it gets customers for you. You enter your website. Origami recommends plays, where each play is a campaign with an audience, an angle and the work to run it. You review the audience, messages and content, approve the play, and Origami runs it.

The engine underneath is search. Each prompt searches more than 50 sources in real time, including Google Maps, social profiles, job boards, company sites and the open web. That reach helps with buyers that static databases miss. Buying signals work as searches too: you can filter by hiring, funding rounds, social posts and tech stack changes. Its industry directory covers more than 130 verticals, from SaaS companies to HVAC contractors and dental practices.

Contact data runs through waterfalls: five email providers and nine phone providers, tried in order. Origami passes provider pricing through at cost and charges nothing when no provider finds a match.

Every paid plan includes the Sequencer, with email and social cadences of up to 10 steps and one shared inbox for replies. Every paid plan also includes unlimited team members. Pro and above add Content mode, which drafts posts with a branded visual. A read-only CRM link with Attio, Salesforce or HubSpot flags leads you already have.

For a team that wants fresh lists on demand at a low entry price, Origami is a sound pick. Our [Origami vs Pancake comparison](/origami-vs-pancake) goes feature by feature.

## Why people look for Origami alternatives

Five reasons come up when founders compare.

- **Credit math.** Origami charges credits for data: new leads, verified emails, verified phones and enrichments. A verified email costs 3 credits and a verified phone 15. Talking to the agent is free, but the monthly bill follows how much data you pull, and top-ups cost $18 to $22 per 1,000 credits.
- **Searching versus watching.** Origami shines when you know what to ask for. Some founders would rather have a tool that watches for buying signals every day and brings them the people, with no new search to start.
- **Inbox upkeep.** Email cadences need mailboxes and domains that stay healthy. Origami sells done-for-you inboxes from $3.20 to $4 a month each. Some teams would rather not run cold email at all.
- **Custom data work.** Teams with a GTM engineer often want to design their own enrichment and scoring across hundreds of providers.
- **One platform for reps.** Larger sales teams want a database, calling, deliverability and analytics in one seat-based tool.

## 1. Pancake: the pick when you want buyer conversations

Pancake is an AI GTM team for founders and small B2B companies. Its AI agents find the people showing buying signals, start the conversations and write articles for search. If you came to Origami for customers and found yourself managing lists, Pancake runs the daily loop from signal to first message.

What it does:

- **Starts from your website.** Pancake reads it and fills its GTM Brain with your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections, then sharpens that memory with every result.
- **Watches six kinds of buying signals.** People posting about a topic you choose, people engaging with a competitor's posts, with a voice your buyers follow or with your own posts, companies hiring for roles that match your buyer, and job posts that name a tool you replace.
- **Hands you leads each morning, with the reason.** Every lead shows the signal behind it. New leads also land in Slack, so you can approve them there or in the app.
- **Opens warm, from your own account.** Outreach runs on the professional network your buyers use: a profile visit, a like on a recent post, an invite with no note, then up to three messages. The first message asks a light question about that signal instead of pitching.
- **Writes for Google and AI answers, and works from your chat.** Pancake drafts articles built to show up in search, and you approve each one. Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Codex through its MCP server to review leads, tune signals or start outreach from the same conversation.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month, flat](/pricing), and that covers every agent. Nothing is metered and there are no seats to add. The 3-day free trial starts with a card on file, and you can cancel anytime.

**How it differs from Origami:** Origami builds a fresh list from live sources for each play or prompt. Pancake watches every day and brings you the people showing buying signals. Origami bills in credits, so cost follows the data you pull, while Pancake costs the same every month. Pancake is built for warm conversations started from your own account, so if your motion runs on verified emails and phone numbers, a data tool like Origami still covers that part.

**Pick it if** you run a B2B company without a sales team and would rather start the day with people already showing a buying signal than write the next search prompt. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. Clay: build your own data workflows

Clay describes itself as infrastructure to get any data, run agentic workflows and launch GTM plays. It brings more than 200 data providers into one place and chains them into waterfalls for better coverage. Its AI research agents, Claygents, research companies and people for you. Clay also tracks signals like job changes, syncs ad audiences and includes a sequencer for email.

**Pricing (September 2026):** a free plan with 100 data credits and 500 actions a month. Launch starts at $185 a month, or $167 a month billed annually, with a 14-day trial. Growth, which adds CRM auto-sync, starts at $495 a month ($446 billed annually). Enterprise pricing is on request.

**How it differs from Origami:** Origami gives you an agent that builds the list from a prompt. Clay gives you a table where you design each enrichment and scoring step yourself. You get more control and more to learn.

**Pick it if** you have someone who enjoys building data workflows and wants to tune every step.

## 3. Apollo: a large database and outreach in one tool

Apollo pairs a database of 240 million contacts and 30 million companies with email sequences, a dialer, deliverability tools and CRM integrations. It adds waterfall enrichment, AI research, lead scoring and website visitor identification. It also connects to AI tools through an MCP server.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the free plan gives 900 credits per seat per year and 2 sequences. Paid seats, billed annually, cost $49 a month on Basic, $79 on Professional and $119 on Organization, which needs at least 3 seats. Basic and Professional come with a 14-day trial, and paying monthly costs more.

**How it differs from Origami:** Apollo searches its own database with filters. Origami searches live sources on each prompt, which helps with local businesses and niche roles. Apollo charges per seat, while Origami includes unlimited team members.

**Pick it if** you want one tool for data, sequences and calling, with a free plan to start.

## 4. Gojiberry: a signal-led agent for email and social

Gojiberry, backed by Y Combinator, sells an AI agent that finds high-intent leads and contacts them for you. Setup starts from your website. The agent detects buying and social signals, such as people engaging with a competitor or following your company, and builds lookalike audiences from your best customers. It scores each prospect against your ideal buyer, then writes and sends outreach by email and on social. You choose full autopilot or approval before each send.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Pro costs $99 a month, with 2 AI agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted a month. It includes email enrichment across 15+ providers and CRM, API and MCP integrations. A custom plan serves teams of 5 or more and outbound agencies. Pro starts with a free trial, and the custom plan with a demo.

**How it differs from Origami:** Gojiberry picks leads from signals and runs the outreach on its own. Origami builds lists from searches and plays you approve. Gojiberry caps volume by people contacted, not by credits. [Gojiberry vs Pancake](/gojiberry-vs-pancake) puts the two side by side.

**Pick it if** you want signal-based outreach across email and social from one agent, at a flat monthly price.

## 5. Valley: outbound that starts on the professional network

Valley calls itself a complete AI outbound system, built for founders, sales teams and lead-gen agencies. It finds leads from signals on the professional network, such as people viewing your profile or company page and people engaging with posts. It qualifies them against your ideal customer, researches each one across 200+ sources and writes messages in your voice, by email and on the professional network. Reply management, Slack alerts and HubSpot and Clay integrations are included. Valley Deep, part of its higher plan, lets you describe a buyer in plain English and find them across the live web. That part is the closest to Origami.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Everything costs $149 a month on quarterly billing, or $199 paid monthly. Everything + Deep costs $419 quarterly or $499 monthly and adds $300 a month of Deep credits. The $1,499-a-month done-for-you service is waitlisted. New accounts get a 7-day free trial.

**How it differs from Origami:** Valley starts from who is already looking at you and your market on the professional network. Origami starts from a search across many sources and builds the list from there.

**Pick it if** your buyers spend their time on the professional network and you want outreach driven by who views and engages with you there.

## 6. Amplemarket: an AI sales platform for teams

Amplemarket is an all-in-one sales platform built around its AI agent, Duo. Duo captures signals in the accounts you care about, researches them, and builds multichannel sequences with email, call and social steps, down to personalized voice notes. Underneath sit a lead search tool, a parallel dialer, an AI inbox for replies and a deliverability kit that warms mailboxes and checks for spam.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Startup runs $600 a month on an annual term and includes 2 users and 27,000 contacts. Growth, with 4 users, and Elite, with 10, are quoted on request. A free trial comes with every plan.

**How it differs from Origami:** Amplemarket is built for sales teams with reps, and its entry plan runs on an annual term with users included. Origami bills monthly by credits and leaves seats uncapped.

**Pick it if** you run a sales team that wants data, sequences, calls and deliverability in one place, and an annual contract suits you.
