Viktor bills in credits. Pancake bills one flat monthly price. Neither charges per seat, so the useful comparison is what each dollar buys and how the bill moves as you use the product more.

This page sets out Viktor's pricing as of September 2026, taken from Viktor's own pricing page. Then it covers Pancake's price, and what each one costs a founder whose main goal is pipeline.

**TL;DR:** Viktor is free to try with up to $100 in credits and no card. Paid plans start at $50 a month for 20,000 credits shared across your workspace, and the bill follows how much work you hand it. Pancake costs $99 a month flat for an AI GTM team, with a 3-day trial that needs a card. Viktor fits a team that wants one general AI employee. Pancake fits a founder whose priority is finding buyers and running outreach.

## Viktor pricing at a glance (as of September 2026)

Viktor's self-serve plan, Team, comes in credit steps, and Enterprise is priced on request. Every step includes every feature and every integration. The only thing that changes between steps is the number of credits.

| Plan | Price per month | Credits per month |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Up to $100 in credits, no credit card |
| Team, entry step | $50 | 20,000 |
| Team | $75 | 30,000 |
| Team, default on the pricing card | $100 | 40,000 |
| Team | $200 | 80,000 |
| Small-company steps | $300 to $5,000 | 125,000 to 2,000,000 |
| Medium-company steps | $7,500 to $30,000 | 3,000,000 to 12,000,000 |
| Enterprise steps | $35,000 to $50,000 | 14,000,000 to 20,000,000 |
| Enterprise, custom | On request | Volume credit pricing, plus invoicing, security review and DPA, dedicated onboarding |

The pricing page title says plans start at $50 a month, but the Team card opens on $100 for 40,000 credits. A selector moves it down to $50 or up the steps.

Almost every step works out to $2.50 per 1,000 credits. The one exception is 125,000 credits for $300, which comes to $2.40. Bigger self-serve steps buy more credits at the same rate. Volume discounts are part of the custom Enterprise plan.

## How Viktor credits work

Viktor says each credit reflects model work done at Anthropic, OpenAI and other providers. Caching lowers the cost of workflows you repeat. The rest of the rules, from its pricing page and FAQ:

- Credits are workspace-based. Your whole team draws from one pool, and there is no per-seat charge.
- Scheduled automations run on credits. A report that runs every morning costs credits every morning.
- Unused monthly plan credits roll over for one month. Free-trial and bonus credits never expire.
- You can top up on demand, turn on auto-top-ups with a monthly spend cap, and change plans at any time.
- Registered nonprofits get 15% off every monthly plan.

Viktor publishes three cost bands for tasks. At $2.50 per 1,000 credits, they convert to dollars like this:

| Task type (Viktor's own examples) | Credits | Dollar cost |
|---|---|---|
| Quick task: Slack summary plus a CRM follow-up | 100–300 | $0.25–$0.75 |
| Complex workflow: a website change, ready to review | 500–1,500 | $1.25–$3.75 |
| Full project: an internal app built across several tools | 2,000–5,000 | $5.00–$12.50 |

Viktor's FAQ adds two reference points. A quick Slack question might cost 50 to 100 credits. And 20,000 credits cover roughly 40 to 200 tasks. Viktor says most solo users find 20,000 to 40,000 credits plenty for a month.

## Is Viktor free?

Viktor has a free tier with up to $100 in credits. It needs no credit card and no sales call. You get every feature and every integration while the credits last. The free credits never expire. When they run out, Viktor asks you to choose a paid Team step, starting at $50 a month.

For trying the product, that's the lowest-friction entry of the two. Pancake has no free plan, and its trial needs a card.

## Pancake pricing

Pancake is an AI GTM team: AI agents that find your buyers and run your outreach. It has [one plan](/pricing):

- $99 per month flat, with every agent included.
- No tiers, no seats, no credits and no usage billing.
- A 3-day free trial. A credit card is required to start it.
- Cancel anytime. Access runs to the end of the billing period.

Here is what the $99 covers:

- **Setup from your website.** Pancake learns your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. It keeps them in one shared memory, the GTM Brain, and keeps learning from what works.
- **Six kinds of buying signals.** People posting about keywords you choose. People engaging with a competitor's posts, an influencer your buyers follow, or your own posts. Companies hiring for roles that match your buyer, and companies whose job posts name a tool, such as one you replace.
- **Leads every morning.** Each lead says which signal picked it. You approve leads in the app or from Slack, where Pancake posts each new one.
- **Outreach from your own account on the professional network.** A profile visit, a like on a recent post, an invite with no note, then up to three messages. The first message asks one light question tied to the signal. It makes no pitch.
- **Articles for Google and AI search.** Pancake writes them. You approve them.

What the plan leaves out matters for budgeting too. Pancake doesn't send email, sync to a CRM or export CSVs. If your pipeline lives in HubSpot or Salesforce, you move leads there yourself.

## What a founder who wants pipeline pays

Say your main goal is more conversations with buyers. The total cost has three parts on either side: the subscription, the other tools involved, and your own time.

### On Viktor

Viktor can take on prospecting and outreach work. Its pricing page lists outreach and pipeline among the jobs credits pay for. You design the workflow: which tools it connects to, what it looks for, what it writes and how often it runs. Every run draws credits.

Viktor's pricing page gives no credit cost for a prospecting run. You can bracket one with its own task bands. The table below assumes one scheduled run per weekday, about 22 runs a month:

| If each run costs like a… | Credits a month | Credit cost a month |
|---|---|---|
| Quick task (100–300 credits) | 2,200–6,600 | $5.50–$16.50 |
| Complex workflow (500–1,500 credits) | 11,000–33,000 | $27.50–$82.50 |
| Full project (2,000–5,000 credits) | 44,000–110,000 | $110–$275 |

Plans come in steps, so you round up to the next one: $50, $75, $100, then $200. Anything else your team asks Viktor to do that month draws from the same pool. Tools Viktor connects to, such as your CRM or a data provider, keep their own subscriptions.

Your time is the third line. You write the instructions, choose the sources and review what comes back. When lead quality drops, you rework the workflow.

### On Pancake

The subscription is a fixed $99 a month. Setup is adding your website. The workflow comes predefined, from signal to lead to outreach, so there is nothing to build.

Your time goes to approving leads and articles. The trade-off is flexibility. Pancake runs those GTM steps and nothing outside them.

### Where the two meet

For light, occasional prospecting, Viktor's $50 step costs less than Pancake. For a daily, multi-step routine, the credit math can pass $99, depending on how heavy each run is. The $49 gap at entry level matters less than two other questions: how much GTM work you want done every day, and whether you want to design it or approve it.

## When Viktor is the better buy

- You want one AI employee across many jobs, from reports and code to ads and sales follow-ups.
- Your team lives in Slack or Microsoft Teams and wants to assign work there.
- Your CRM and other tools need updating as part of the work.
- You want to try before entering a card.
- Your usage is light or uneven, and you prefer paying for what you use.

## When Pancake is the better buy

- Your main goal is pipeline: finding buyers and starting conversations with them.
- You want a fixed monthly cost with no credit balance to watch.
- You'd rather approve leads and articles than build prospecting workflows.
- Your buyers are active on the professional network, where Pancake runs its outreach.

## Bottom line

As of September 2026, Viktor costs nothing to start, then $50 a month and up in credit steps shared across your workspace. It's the more flexible buy if you want one AI employee for many kinds of work and you're ready to direct it.

Pancake is one $99 plan, and the bill doesn't move. If pipeline is the job, weigh that $99 against the Viktor step your prospecting routine would need, plus the hours you'd spend building and tuning it.
