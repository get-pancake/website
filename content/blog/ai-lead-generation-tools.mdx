## Four jobs, and which one to buy first

AI lead generation software does one of four jobs. Most lead with one and add pieces of the others.

1. **Signals: who is in market now.** A job post, a funding round, a visit to your pricing page, a public post about the problem. The AI matches and filters.
2. **Data and enrichment: who they are and how to reach them.** Names, roles, work emails, phone numbers. The AI researches and fills gaps.
3. **Sequencing: sending and following up.** Mailboxes, warm-up, follow-ups that stop on a reply. The AI writes variants and sorts replies.
4. **Agents: several steps in a row.** Find people, check fit, write, send, inside rules you set.

Buy for your bottleneck. No list, buy data. A list and no time to send, buy a sequencer. No time for either, look at an agent. [AI for sales prospecting](/blog/ai-for-sales-prospecting) splits the same work by what to hand off and what to keep.

## Signal tools: who is in market now

Signal tools watch for the moments that make a company likelier to buy. The AI work is matching signals to your profile and filtering out noise.

### Unify: signals, data and sequences, priced per seat

Unify pairs a database of 1.1 billion people and 65 million companies with signals such as open roles, tech stack, funding and social activity, then writes and sends multichannel sequences.

**Price (Nov 2026):** free for up to three seats with limited credits. Base costs $20 per seat a month with 800 credits, and Pro $60 with 2,400 credits and a 14-day trial, no card ([Unify pricing](https://www.unifygtm.com/pricing)).

**Pick it if** you want signals, data and sending in one seat.

### Common Room: every signal about one buyer in one place

Common Room joins job changes, social and community engagement, website activity and Bombora intent topics into one view per person and account. RoomieAI researches accounts, AI scoring ranks them, and every plan includes an MCP server.

**Price (Nov 2026):** the entry plan costs $2,500 a month billed annually, with five seats and up to 100,000 contacts ([Common Room pricing](https://www.commonroom.io/pricing/)).

**Pick it if** your team collects signals in many places and wants them joined per buyer.

### RB2B: who is on your website

RB2B names the companies behind visits to your site worldwide, and the people in the US, then posts them to Slack or Teams. Pro adds business emails and integrations with HubSpot, Salesforce, Clay and Apollo.

**Price (Nov 2026):** the free plan resolves 150 visits a month at company level. Starter costs $79 a month and Pro starts at $149, with a 7-day trial of Pro ([RB2B pricing](https://www.rb2b.com/pricing)).

**Pick it if** your site draws steady B2B traffic. Pancake doesn't identify website visitors, so the two don't overlap.

### Octolens: public posts that mention your space

Octolens tracks keywords across Reddit, X, Hacker News, YouTube, GitHub, Stack Overflow and more. AI scores each mention for relevance, and alerts go to Slack or email, with an API and an MCP server.

**Price (Nov 2026):** Pro costs $159 a month billed annually for 15,000 mentions and 10 keywords, with a 7-day trial and no card ([Octolens pricing](https://octolens.com/pricing)).

**Pick it if** your buyers ask for tool recommendations in public threads.

## Data and enrichment tools: who they are and how to reach them

The AI here shows up as research agents, filters and scores.

### Apollo: a database, sequences and AI scoring under one login

Apollo combines contact and company data with AI research, AI lead scoring, intent topics, sequences and a dialer. [Apollo alternatives](/blog/apollo-alternatives) compares it with the other all-in-one tools.

**Price (Nov 2026):** the free plan gives 900 credits per seat per year. Basic costs $49 per seat a month billed annually, or $65 monthly, with a 14-day trial. Lead scoring is capped on Free and Basic and unlimited on Professional, at $79 ([Apollo pricing](https://www.apollo.io/pricing)).

**Pick it if** you want data, sending and scoring in one place, with a free plan to test.

### Clay: enrichment you build yourself

Clay chains more than 150 data providers into waterfalls, so a lookup falls through to the next provider when one has no answer. Claygent, its AI agent, researches the web to fill custom columns. Launch adds job-change and company-news signals and an email sequencer. [Clay alternatives](/blog/clay-alternatives) covers tools that need less building.

**Price (Nov 2026):** the free plan includes 6,000 actions and 1,200 data credits a year. Launch costs $167 a month billed annually, or $185 monthly, with a 14-day trial ([Clay pricing](https://www.clay.com/pricing)).

**Pick it if** someone on your team enjoys building tables.

### Lusha: contacts for a list you already have

Lusha sells verified contacts, company data and buying signals in its web app, a browser extension, an API and an MCP server.

**Price (Nov 2026):** free for 40 credits a month with no card. Starter costs $37.45 a month billed yearly for 4,800 credits a year ([Lusha pricing](https://www.lusha.com/pricing/)).

**Pick it if** you need phone numbers and emails for names you found elsewhere.

## Sequencing tools: sending and follow-ups at volume

Sequencers run the mailboxes, the warm-up, the follow-ups and the stop on reply, and their AI writes variants and sorts replies. Pancake doesn't send email, so an email-first team needs one of these either way.

### Instantly: many mailboxes, one price

Every Instantly plan includes unlimited email accounts and warm-up. Growth covers 1,000 uploaded contacts and 5,000 emails a month, and AI agents handle replies and lead finding.

**Price (Nov 2026):** Growth costs $47 a month, Hypergrowth $107 ([Instantly pricing](https://instantly.ai/pricing)).

**Pick it if** email is your main channel and you send from many mailboxes.

### Smartlead: the low-cost sender

Smartlead's Base plan covers 2,000 contacts, 6,000 sends and 2,000 verified emails a month. Add-ons buy and set up mailboxes for you, from $3.99 per mailbox a month.

**Price (Nov 2026):** Base costs $39 a month, or $32 billed yearly ([Smartlead pricing](https://www.smartlead.ai/pricing)).

**Pick it if** you send at volume on a tight budget, or run sending for clients.

### lemlist: sequences plus a lead database

The lemlist Email plan includes unlimited users, 50,000 emails a month, a database of more than 650 million leads, and lemAgent, which builds sequences from a chat. Signal agents and an MCP server come with it. Our [lemlist alternatives guide](/blog/lemlist-alternatives) compares it with seven rivals.

**Price (Nov 2026):** Email costs $69 a month, or $55 billed yearly. Multichannel, which adds social steps, SMS and calling, costs $109 per user ([lemlist pricing](https://www.lemlist.com/pricing)).

**Pick it if** you want one flat price for a whole team's email sequences.

## AI agents: tools that run several steps in a row

A general assistant can plan prospecting. Claude has connectors on every plan, and Pro costs $20 a month or $17 billed annually ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). On its own it has no lead data and can't send under your name. The agents below bring both.

### Gojiberry: one agent across email and social

Gojiberry reads your website, then its agent watches signals such as engagement with competitors, scores prospects against your ideal buyer and contacts them by email and social, on full auto or with a review step. Pro includes two agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted a month.

**Price (Nov 2026):** plans start at $99 a month for Pro, with custom plans above and a free trial ([Gojiberry pricing](https://gojiberry.ai/#pricing)).

**Pick it if** you want one agent to run outbound across two channels.

### AiSDR: pay per person contacted

AiSDR's agent, Ami, builds and runs multichannel sequences on 2 billion profiles, with signals such as job changes, new hires and funding, and enrichment from 20 providers. It classifies and answers replies.

**Price (Nov 2026):** $1.50 per person prospected on pay as you go, stop anytime ([AiSDR pricing](https://aisdr.com/pricing/)).

**Pick it if** you want to test an AI-run sequence without a subscription.

### Artisan: an AI BDR on a free or annual plan

Artisan's Ava finds leads, runs email and social outreach, handles replies, books meetings and queues calls for your reps. The [Artisan alternatives guide](/blog/artisan-alternatives) covers its closest rivals.

**Price (Nov 2026):** free for 300 credits a month with no card. Intern costs $250 a month billed annually, for about 500 leads contacted a month ([Artisan pricing](https://www.artisan.co/pricing)).

**Pick it if** you want a hands-off AI BDR and can commit to a year.

### Amplemarket: data and AI for a sales team

Amplemarket combines its own database with AI intent signals, multichannel sequences and Duo, an AI assistant that drafts outreach, plus an MCP add-on for Claude or ChatGPT.

**Price (Nov 2026):** Startup costs $600 a month on an annual term, with two users and 27,000 contacts, and a free trial ([Amplemarket pricing](https://www.amplemarket.com/pricing)).

**Pick it if** you run a funded team of reps and want data, signals and sending in one contract.

## Pick by the sentence that sounds like you

- **"I don't know who to contact."** A signal tool, or Apollo's free plan.
- **"I have names, no emails."** Lusha or Clay.
- **"I have a list and no time to send."** Smartlead, Instantly or lemlist.
- **"I want new leads and first conversations without running a stack."** An agent, chosen by budget and channel.

## Pancake: buying signals to a first conversation, run from your agent

Pancake is our product, so weigh this entry with that in mind. It reads your website to build a GTM Brain (your offer, ideal customers, competitors and voice), then each Play turns one sentence into a search that runs on the schedule you pick. Plays find people posting about a topic, people engaging with a rival's or an expert's posts, companies hiring a role, tools named in job posts, recently funded companies, lookalikes of your best customer, and facts a company states publicly, with the source linked.

Say you sell a customer-feedback tool to product teams. A Play could read: "Find heads of product at US software companies with 50 to 500 people who comment on a rival feedback tool's posts." Each lead comes with one line on why it fits, and the first message asks a question instead of pitching: "Saw your comment on the post about feature voting. How does your team decide what makes the roadmap?"

It runs from Claude, ChatGPT or Codex over MCP, with about 100 tools and a browser sign-in ([setup for Claude](/claude)). Messages go out as DMs under your name. It sends no email, makes no calls and can't see which tools a website runs. Pricing starts at $99 a month per workspace with 60,000 credits and free teammates ([pricing](/pricing)), and a card starts the 7-day trial with 30,000 credits.
