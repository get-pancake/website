## What PAIR was

PAIR was an AI co-founder built around conversation. It held your company context and helped you think through decisions, draft communications and research questions.

Its website, getpair.app, no longer resolves. We can't say why, and we won't guess. Every tool below was live and open to new users when we checked in September 2026.

## 1. Pancake: our pick if you need customers

PAIR helped you think. Pancake goes out and finds your buyers. It is an AI GTM team built for founders and small B2B companies, and its agents handle the prospecting and open the conversations.

Setup is your website. From it, Pancake works out how you position the product, who your ideal customers are, what you offer, the proof you can show and the objections you run into. That picture lives in the GTM Brain, a shared memory every agent works from, and each result refines it.

Six kinds of buying signal feed the search. They range from someone posting about a topic you pick to a company whose job ads mention a tool you replace. Each morning, a new set of leads is waiting, tagged with the signal behind every one. Approve them in the app, or from the Slack message Pancake posts for each lead.

For every lead you approve, Pancake reaches out from your own profile on the professional social network. It visits the profile, likes something they posted recently, sends a connection invite without a note, and follows with up to three messages. Message one carries no pitch: a single light question about the signal that flagged them.

If talking to an AI was what you liked about PAIR, keep doing it. Pancake runs a hosted MCP server. Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Codex with a browser sign-in, then ask it to pull up the GTM Brain, go through the morning's leads, change which signals Pancake watches or launch outreach, all in the same conversation.

Pancake writes articles too, aimed at Google results and AI answers, and none goes live until you approve it. The price is $99 a month, flat. That covers every agent, with no per-seat charge and nothing metered. You can try it free for 3 days; the trial asks for a card.

**Best for:** founders and small B2B companies with a working product and too few buyer conversations.

## 2. CoFounder.AI: the closest match to PAIR

CoFounder.AI is built around the idea PAIR had: one AI partner that keeps your priorities and strategy in view. Behind that partner sits a bench of specialists covering growth, product, design, sales, operations and finance, and it routes each job to the right one. Voice mode is on every plan, a good fit if you think out loud. More than 50 integrations, GitHub, Stripe, HubSpot, Slack and Notion among them, let it work inside the tools you use.

You pay in monthly credits (September 2026 prices): $39 for Starter with 3,000 credits, $99 for Pro with 9,000, and $399 for Max with 27,000. Pro and Max add $9 a month for each human teammate. There is no free plan on the site.

**Best for:** founders who want a partner to talk to, with specialists who can carry the work further.

## 3. Copreneur.ai: a daily focus partner

Copreneur.ai advises; it does not run tasks for you. It opens each day with your priorities, checks goals against their deadlines, researches your market and warns you when your work drifts off course. It also works as a sounding board for brainstorming and pressure-testing decisions, the part nearest to PAIR's thinking-partner role.

Plans in September 2026: Starter at $25 a month covers one goal, two projects and ten tasks. Pro at $49 allows unlimited goals and projects and connects your email and calendar. Team at $99 adds shared goals. All three come with a 7-day free trial.

**Best for:** founders spread across several projects who need a clear daily focus.

## 4. VenturOS: an AI executive team for technical founders

VenturOS, founded in January 2026 and based in Dubai, hands you a named AI executive team. Venos runs point as chief of staff, with Cole on operations, Ren on tech, Lyra on product, Vivi on marketing and Rio on growth. Cato is the mentor, the seat closest to a PAIR-style sounding board. The team learns your product, stack and conventions from your GitHub repositories.

Drafts happen on their own. Nothing publishes and no money moves until you approve, and calls on pricing, hiring and commitments stay yours. Early access is free and needs no card as of September 2026. Paid plans are on the way: Core at $200 a month, and Operator at $500 a month for recurring work the team runs itself.

**Best for:** small technical teams, up to five people, who want the drafting done and the last word on anything that goes out.

## How to choose a PAIR replacement

Start from what you used PAIR for.

- **Working out who to sell to.** The next step is reaching those people, and that is Pancake's job. It finds them through buying signals and opens each conversation from your own account.
- **Talking problems through.** CoFounder.AI keeps the one-partner feel and adds voice.
- **Deciding what matters today.** Copreneur.ai sets your priorities and flags drift.
- **Drafting from your codebase.** VenturOS reads your repositories and asks before anything ships.

A general assistant such as Claude or ChatGPT also covers part of what PAIR did, though it leaves company context and follow-through to you. Connect it to Pancake's MCP server, and the chat where you plan can also start outreach to your buyers.
