**TL;DR:** Amplemarket is a strong all-in-one platform for sales teams: data, buying signals, multichannel sequences, a dialer and an AI layer called Duo. If you are a founder or a small B2B team and you want buyers and conversations, pick Pancake: its AI agents find the people showing buying signals and start the conversation, for $99 a month flat. Apollo sells the same all-in-one model for less. lemlist suits teams that run their own campaigns, and Artisan suits teams that want an AI BDR. Valley, Gojiberry and Dripify cost less and lean on social outreach.

## What Amplemarket does well

Amplemarket puts the outbound stack in one product. Its Searcher finds contacts and companies with dozens of filters. Its signals flag website visits, job changes, people engaging with a competitor, bad reviews of a competitor and posts about your space. Its sequences mix email, calls, social steps and AI voice notes. A parallel dialer, a unified inbox and a deliverability suite with warmup and domain monitoring complete the set.

Duo, which Amplemarket calls an AI sales copilot, reads your website, the web and your CRM. From them it builds a shared library of personas, competitors, case studies and objection handling. It turns signals into personalized sequences and drafts replies to prospects. Autopilot can enroll signal leads into a sequence as soon as they appear, and every plan includes AI agents that research prospects and personalize outreach.

It syncs both ways with Salesforce and HubSpot. An MCP add-on lets reps prospect and research from Claude or ChatGPT. Reviews on its own site keep praising a clean, easy interface.

For a sales team with several reps, many mailboxes and a CRM to keep in sync, Amplemarket is a sound pick.

## Why people look for Amplemarket alternatives

Five reasons come up when teams compare.

- **The entry price.** The Startup plan is $600 a month on an annual term, so $7,200 for the year. Growth and Elite are quoted by sales.
- **It is built for a sales team.** Extra seats, a parallel dialer and mailboxes per user fit a team of SDRs. A founder selling alone runs all of it personally.
- **Mailboxes and domains to babysit.** Multichannel sequences depend on extra mailboxes, domains and warmup. Some founders would rather reach buyers where they already post and comment.
- **It still needs an operator.** Duo, the AI agents and autopilot speed up the work. Someone still approves the sequences, looks after the mailboxes and answers the replies. Some teams want agents to carry more of that.
- **A cheaper way in.** Teams that want a free plan or monthly billing look at self-serve tools.

## 1. Pancake: the founder's pick for buyers and conversations

Pancake is an AI GTM team for founders and small B2B companies. Amplemarket hands a sales team the tools. Pancake's agents do the prospecting and open the conversations themselves, so there is no platform for you to run.

- **A brain built from your website.** Duo builds its library from your site, the web and your CRM. Pancake reads your website and turns it into the GTM Brain: who you sell to, what you offer, your proof and the objections you hear. It gets sharper with every result.
- **Signals you choose.** Pancake watches six kinds: a topic people post about, engagement with a rival's posts, with a voice your buyers follow or with your own posts, companies with open roles that match your buyer, and job posts that name a tool you replace.
- **A short list each morning.** New leads land in the app and in Slack, each with the signal that surfaced it. You approve the ones you want.
- **A warm first touch.** Outreach runs from your own account on the professional network. Pancake visits the person's profile, likes one of their recent posts, sends an invite without a note, and follows with up to three messages. The opening message skips the pitch and asks a single light question tied to that signal.
- **Articles and your AI tools.** Pancake also writes articles aimed at Google and at AI answers, and you sign off on each one. If you work in Claude, ChatGPT or Codex, its MCP server lets you check leads, tune signals and launch outreach without leaving the chat.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month, flat](/pricing), all agents included, with no seats and no usage billing. You can cancel anytime. The 3-day free trial asks for a card up front.

**How it differs from Amplemarket:** Amplemarket gives a sales team a platform to operate: a database, sequences, a dialer and deliverability tools. Pancake's agents carry the work from signal to first message, with no mailboxes or domains to warm up. Amplemarket starts at $600 a month on a yearly term. Pancake costs $99 a month with no seats. Pancake's conversations happen on the professional network rather than by email, so if cold email and calls carry your pipeline, Apollo or lemlist fit that motion better.

**Pick it if** you sell without SDRs and your goal is conversations with the right buyers. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. Apollo: the closest like-for-like platform

Apollo is an all-in-one sales platform built on a database of 240 million contacts and 30 million companies. It covers prospecting, sequences, a deliverability suite with warmup, a dialer, intent topics, website visitor identification and deal management. AI research, lead scoring and agents sit on top, and Apollo has its own MCP server for AI assistants.

**Pricing (September 2026):** free forever with 900 credits per seat a year and two sequences. Paid plans are per seat on annual billing: Basic at $49 a month, Professional at $79, and Organization at $119 with at least three seats. Basic and Professional each offer a 14-day trial.

**How it differs from Amplemarket:** the same shape at a lower, self-serve entry point. You can start free and add seats one at a time. Agents come with Professional and above.

**Pick it if** you like Amplemarket's all-in-one model and want it for less, with a free plan to start.

## 3. lemlist: multichannel campaigns you run yourself

With lemlist, a single sequence runs across email, the professional network, calls, SMS and WhatsApp. It comes with a lead database of more than 650 million contacts, email and phone finders, a Deliverability Hub with warmup, and AI agents that watch intent signals, enrich data and build campaigns (lemAgent). Developers get an MCP server and a CLI.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Email plan is $55 a month on yearly billing ($69 monthly) and covers unlimited users. Multichannel is $87 per user a month on yearly billing ($109 monthly). Data enrichment ($16 a month, yearly) and buying-intent signals ($21 a month, yearly) are add-ons. The 14-day trial asks for no card.

**How it differs from Amplemarket:** similar channels, self-serve plans and monthly billing. You own the campaigns: lemAgent helps build them, and your team runs them.

**Pick it if** you want to keep running your own multichannel campaigns for less. Our [lemlist vs Pancake comparison](/lemlist-vs-pancake) covers the trade-off.

## 4. Artisan: an AI BDR that handles the replies

Artisan sells Ava, an AI BDR. Ava searches more than 250 million B2B contacts or your CRM, enriches them from 22+ sources and ranks them with intent signals such as funding rounds and leadership hires. She sends personalized outreach across email and social, queues call steps for your reps, handles replies and objections, and books meetings for your reps. Ava runs on her own by default, and you can switch on approvals to review what she sends. Artisan is SOC 2 Type II certified.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Free plan gives 300 credits a month for lead search and lists, with no campaigns. Intern is $250 a month billed annually for 10,000 credits a month, which Artisan puts at about 500 leads contacted. Employee is $600 a month billed annually for 25,000 credits, about 1,250 leads. Bigger plans go through sales. New accounts get 30 days and 10,000 credits to try it, with no card.

**How it differs from Amplemarket:** Duo helps your reps write and reply. Ava runs the motion herself, replies and booking included, and billing follows credits.

**Pick it if** you want an AI BDR to run outbound end to end and credit-based billing suits you.

## 5. Valley: outreach to people already in your orbit

Valley starts from people who have already noticed you. It tracks who viewed your profile, engaged with your posts or followed your company page on the professional network, and it can search for people who fit your ideal customer. It scores each lead against that profile, researches it and writes invites and messages in your voice. HubSpot, Pipedrive and Clay integrations are included.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Everything is $149 a month billed quarterly or $199 month to month, for one seat. On that plan, tracking profile viewers, followers and post engagers is a $50-a-month add-on. Everything + Deep is $419 a month billed quarterly ($499 monthly), includes that tracking and adds $300 a month of Deep search credits. A 7-day free trial is available.

**How it differs from Amplemarket:** Valley is social-first and priced per seat. Amplemarket covers more channels and adds a contact database and a dialer.

**Pick it if** your profile and posts already draw buyers and you want to reach them first.

## 6. Gojiberry: a signal-based outreach agent

Gojiberry, backed by Y Combinator, reads your website to learn what you sell and who buys it. Its agent detects buying and social signals, such as engagement with a competitor or a follow of your company. It builds lookalikes of your best customers and scores prospects against your ideal buyer. It then runs outreach over email and social, fully automatic or with approval before sending, and drafts your replies. It connects to HubSpot, Pipedrive and Claude.

**Pricing (September 2026):** a single Pro plan at $99 a month covers two AI agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted each month. Sales teams of five or more and outbound agencies get a custom plan. You can start on a free trial.

**How it differs from Amplemarket:** the agent sets itself up from your website, with no sequences to build. It is priced flat and aimed at founders and operators, while Amplemarket adds a dialer and tooling for larger sales teams.

**Pick it if** you want an agent to run social and email outreach from signals. We compare it with Pancake in [Gojiberry vs Pancake](/gojiberry-vs-pancake).

## 7. Dripify: the lowest-cost sequencer

Dripify automates steps on the professional network and by email: profile views, invites, messages, likes, follows and emails, with delays and conditions. It adds an email finder, AI icebreakers, a dedicated inbox from the Pro plan, multi-team management on Advanced and account-safety settings.

**Pricing (September 2026):** per user, Basic is $39 a month billed annually ($59 monthly), Pro $59 ($79) and Advanced $79 ($99). All plans get 100 email finder credits a month. The 7-day trial runs without a card.

**How it differs from Amplemarket:** Dripify is a sequencer first. You choose the audience and write the steps. Amplemarket adds signals, a contact database and Duo on top.

**Pick it if** price matters most, you already know who to target, and the professional network is your main channel.
