**Pancake and Crevio both use AI to take work off a founder's plate. They do not compete.** Crevio builds digital storefronts for creators: the infrastructure to sell a course, a download, or a membership. [Pancake](/) is an AI GTM team for B2B companies. Its agents find people who show buying signals and run outreach to them. If you are choosing between the two, you are solving two different problems.

**TL;DR:** Crevio is where you go to launch a digital product business with no technical setup. Pancake is where you go when you sell to other businesses and need more buyer conversations. Most founders need one or the other, and the case for using both is narrow.

---

## What Crevio Actually Does

Crevio is an AI business builder for digital product creators. Its pitch: describe what you want to sell, and Crevio builds the storefront, sets up payment processing, creates product pages, and handles email marketing. As of June 2026, 2,401 businesses have launched on the platform.

The target user is a creator, coach, or educator who has expertise and an audience but does not want to configure a tech stack. Crevio handles the commerce infrastructure — Stripe-powered payments, checkout flows, course hosting, customer management — so the creator can focus on producing content.

Crevio places itself at L2-L3 on the autonomy scale: AI that builds the business and handles recurring marketing tasks, while the owner stays in the loop for strategy. It is honest about this. Their own blog describes Crevio as "the most useful version of the autonomous company vision that actually ships today" — not a fully self-directing system, but a commercially viable one.

The product is built around digital goods. Courses, downloads, memberships, coaching packages. If you are a SaaS founder or a company with a service business and employees, Crevio is not the right tool. Their own comparison guide sends those users elsewhere.

## What Pancake Does

You start by adding your website. Pancake builds its GTM Brain from it: who you sell to, what you offer, the proof you have, and the objections you hear. It keeps learning from what works.

Its agents watch for buying signals. A person posts about a topic you picked. Someone engages with a competitor's posts, or with an influencer your buyers follow. A company opens a role that matches your buyer, or names a tool you replace in a job post. Each of those can become a lead, delivered in the morning with the signal that triggered it.

You approve leads in the app or in Slack. Pancake then runs the outreach from your own account on the professional social network, and the first message is a light question tied to the signal, with no pitch. It also writes articles meant to rank in Google and show up in AI answers, and you approve those as well.

The price is [$99/month flat](/pricing), every agent included, with a 3-day free trial that needs a credit card to start.

## The Core Difference: Launching a Store vs Finding Buyers

| Dimension | Crevio | Pancake |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Primary use case** | Launch a digital product business | Find B2B buyers and run outreach |
| **Target user** | Creators, coaches, educators | Founders and small B2B companies |
| **Business type** | Digital products (courses, downloads, memberships) | Companies that sell to other businesses |
| **What runs on its own** | Storefront + marketing on autopilot (L2-L3 by Crevio's own framing) | Lead finding and outreach; you approve leads and articles |
| **Where it lives** | Crevio-hosted storefront + email | The Pancake app, Slack, and your own professional-network account |
| **Agent scope** | Commerce, marketing, customer email | Buying signals, outreach, articles for Google and AI search |
| **Payment handling** | Built-in (Stripe-powered) | Not offered |
| **Entry price** | Free tier with a 5% transaction fee | $99/month flat after a 3-day trial |
| **Best for** | Someone starting a creator business from scratch | A B2B company with an offer that needs more buyer conversations |

The simplest test: do you sell to businesses and need more of them talking to you? Pancake fits. Are you trying to launch a digital product and need the commerce infrastructure to sell it? Crevio is the right tool.

## Where Crevio Outperforms Pancake

Crevio wins on launch speed for digital product creators. If you have a course or a download and you want to be selling it within the hour, Crevio's setup time is measured in minutes. There is no tool integration, no agent configuration, and no Slack workspace to connect. You describe the product, Crevio builds the page.

The free tier with a 5% transaction fee also lowers the entry bar. You can validate a product idea before spending anything on the platform. Pancake has no equivalent: it is a flat monthly subscription after a short trial.

Crevio also handles payments natively. Stripe checkout, revenue management, and payout processing are built in. If payment infrastructure is the gap you are solving, Crevio closes it without needing you to wire anything together.

---

## Where Pancake Outperforms Crevio

Pancake wins when the buyer is a business. Crevio's marketing tools are built to sell to an audience the creator already has. Pancake goes looking for people who don't know you yet but recently showed a signal, like posting about the problem you solve or engaging with a rival's posts.

The business model differs too. Crevio is built for digital products sold to consumers. Pancake is built for B2B companies such as SaaS startups, agencies, and consultancies, where a sale starts with a conversation rather than a checkout page.

The memory layer is also different. Pancake's GTM Brain holds your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof, and objections, and it updates as results come in. Crevio's AI generates based on what you tell it in the moment; it is not building a model of your buyers over months of use.

## The Scenario Where You Use Both

There is one realistic sequence for both. A coach launches a course on Crevio, then starts selling leadership programs to companies. The course keeps selling through Crevio's storefront. The company deals start with conversations, and that is where Pancake helps: it finds the founders and team leads showing signals and opens those conversations for you. If that is your path, see how Pancake works for [executive coaches](/for/executive-coaches).

The sequencing matters. Crevio gets a product on sale. Pancake starts once there is an offer and the buyers are businesses. If every sale is a consumer checking out on your store, you don't need Pancake at all.
