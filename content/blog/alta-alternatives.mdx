## What Alta does well

Alta calls itself an AI GTM System of Actions. The work is split between three named agents, which Alta says share one Company Brain, a model of how your go-to-market runs:

- **Katie, outbound.** Finds prospects from more than 50 data sources, spots buying signals and runs personalized outreach.
- **Alex, inbound.** Qualifies inbound leads through AI calls and chat, scores intent and routes ready buyers to your team's calendar.
- **Luna, growth.** Connects your CRM and data sources to detect signals, build lookalikes and surface high-intent accounts for the other two.

Outreach runs across email, the professional network, SMS, WhatsApp and calls, and the next step branches on what the prospect did. Alta lists six motions, from outbound pipeline to no-show prevention and closed-lost revival. It sits on top of the stack you already run: Salesforce, HubSpot and Attio are among its CRM integrations, and Clay is one of more than 50 other tools it connects to. It lists SOC 2 and ISO 27001 compliance.

The traction is real. Alta announced a $25M Series A led by IN Venture in July 2026. The company says it reached its first million in revenue within months of starting to sell and is on track for 800% revenue growth this year. It names Snowflake, Deel and Atlassian among its customers.

For a revenue team with reps, inbound volume and a CRM to keep full, Alta is a serious pick.

## Why people look for Alta alternatives

Five things send teams looking, and most follow from how Alta sells.

- **No public price.** Alta's pricing page asks for your team size and goals, then promises a customized proposal. You can't see a number before a sales conversation.
- **A sales-led start.** You book a demo and get white-glove onboarding. Founders who want to start this week look for something they can switch on themselves.
- **Built for a revenue team.** Alta's pitch is meetings booked so your team can focus on closing. Routing, no-show prevention and closed-lost revival assume reps and an existing pipeline. A founder selling alone needs fewer moving parts.
- **Five channels to run.** Email, SMS, WhatsApp, calls and social messages mean more channels to set up and watch. Some founders would rather open a few warm conversations from their own account.
- **One job, not six.** Some teams need Alex's job and nothing else: converting inbound. A dedicated inbound tool can fit that better.

## 1. Pancake: the pick for founders and small B2B teams who want buyers

Pancake is an AI GTM team whose agents find your buyers and start the conversations for you. Alta hands a revenue team three agents to run. Pancake is built for the founder who is still the revenue team. Each morning it picks the people worth talking to and makes the first move.

- **Reads your business first.** Point Pancake at your website and it fills its GTM Brain with your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. Results feed back in, so the picks get sharper over time.
- **Watches six signals.** Posts about a topic you choose. Engagement with a rival's posts, with a voice your buyers follow, or with your own. Open roles that match your buyer, and job posts that name a tool you replace.
- **Hands you leads each morning, reason attached.** Each one names the signal that surfaced it. New leads land in Slack too, so you can approve them there or in the app.
- **Starts warm, from your account.** Pancake visits the profile, likes a recent post and sends an invite with no note, then follows up with up to three messages. The opener skips the pitch and asks one light question about the signal it spotted.
- **Writes for search and runs from your chat.** Its articles are built to rank in Google and surface in AI answers, and each one waits for your approval. Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Codex through the MCP server to read the GTM Brain, review leads, tune signals and launch outreach without leaving the conversation.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month flat](/pricing) for every agent in one plan, with no seats and no usage billing. A 3-day free trial starts with a card, and you can cancel anytime.

**How it differs from Alta:** Alta covers outbound, inbound and expansion for a sales team across five channels, on a custom quote. Pancake owns the top of the funnel for a founder. It spots the people showing buying signals, opens a warm conversation with each one and writes articles aimed at Google and AI search. You can start today, without a demo. Pancake is built for finding new buyers, so if answering inbound leads by voice is your priority, Alta or Qualified covers that part.

**Pick it if** you sell without a sales team, want more conversations with buyers and want to know the monthly price before you start. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. 11x: the closest like-for-like swap

11x sells what it calls digital workers. Alice handles outbound. She researches prospects, writes personalized outreach by email and social, handles replies and books meetings. Julian handles inbound. He qualifies leads in real time by phone, website chat, SMS and WhatsApp, and you can talk to him live on the 11x site. Pro plans add outreach in more than 105 languages.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Alice starts at $3,750 a month on the Growth plan, billed annually, for up to 5 users and 2,000 new prospects a month. Julian starts at $5,333 a month for voice or $2,417 for chat, also billed annually. Pro and Enterprise are custom quotes, and annual contracts are the default.

**How it differs from Alta:** the shape is the same. Both split the work between named agents for outbound and inbound and sell through a sales call. 11x publishes its entry prices, where Alta quotes. Julian works your phone lines, chat and texts. Alta adds a third agent for signals and lookalikes, plus motions like no-show prevention.

**Pick it if** you run a sales team at a larger company, can sign an annual contract and want Alta's shape from another vendor, especially for inbound calls.

## 3. Artisan: an AI BDR with a free way in

Artisan sells Ava, an AI BDR. Ava finds leads in a database of more than 250 million verified B2B contacts, writes and sends outreach by email and social, handles replies and books meetings. Calls are queued for your reps, with a power dialer as a paid add-on. Ava sends in your reps' names from your own domains, and you can require approval before anything goes out.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Free plan costs $0, with 300 credits a month for finding and enriching leads. Intern costs $250 a month billed annually, with 10,000 credits, or about 500 leads contacted a month. Employee costs $600 a month billed annually, with 25,000 credits. A new account starts with a 30-day trial: 10,000 credits, no card. Larger plans are priced with sales.

**How it differs from Alta:** one named agent focused on outbound, where Alta runs three. Artisan publishes its startup prices. Credits drive the bill, so cost follows volume.

**Pick it if** you like the named-agent model, want outbound at volume and would rather start without a sales call. Our [Artisan alternatives](/blog/artisan-alternatives) guide covers the next step if Ava isn't the fit.

## 4. Amplemarket: one platform for a sales team

Amplemarket is an AI sales platform. Its agent, Duo, captures signals in the accounts you care about, builds personalized sequences with email, call and social steps, and drafts replies for your reps to approve. The platform around Duo adds a B2B contact database, a dialer, deliverability tools and analytics. An MCP add-on lets reps prospect and act from Claude or ChatGPT.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Startup costs $600 a month on an annual term, with 2 users included. Growth and Elite are custom, with 4 and 10 users included. Every plan includes a free trial.

**How it differs from Alta:** Amplemarket is built around reps. Duo proposes the work and people approve it. Alta's agents run the motion themselves. Amplemarket also shows its entry price, where Alta quotes.

**Pick it if** you have several reps and want data, sequences, calling and deliverability in one seat-based tool.

## 5. AiSDR: an AI SDR without a long contract

AiSDR runs researched outreach by email and on the professional network. It sends from Gmail or Outlook, syncs both ways with HubSpot and includes Ami, an AI strategist that proposes campaigns and reads your results. The Explore and Scale plans add social signals, such as post engagement, profile visits and keyword tracking, plus website visitor tracking.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Solo costs $250 a month, month-to-month, for 200 AI-researched contacts a month and one user. Explore costs $900 a month and Scale $2,500, both on quarterly contracts. Annual billing takes 20% off. There is no free trial.

**How it differs from Alta:** self-serve with public prices, where Alta starts with a quote. It runs two channels, email and social, against Alta's five.

**Pick it if** you sell alone or with a small team and want an AI SDR on email and social with no annual commitment.

## 6. Valley: social-first outbound

Valley is an AI outbound system built on social signals. It tracks who views your profile, engages with your posts and follows you. It qualifies each lead against your ideal customer profile, researches them across 200+ sources and writes messages in your voice, sent by email and on the professional network. You approve each message or let them send.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Everything plan is $149 a month on quarterly billing, or $199 paid monthly. Everything + Deep is $419 a month quarterly ($499 monthly) and adds $300 a month of Deep credits for live-web lead search. Valley's done-for-you service is $1,499 a month. A 7-day free trial comes first.

**How it differs from Alta:** Valley starts from signals around your own profile and posts, where Alta pulls from 50+ data sources and your CRM. Valley is self-serve at a flat monthly price.

**Pick it if** your buyers are active on the professional network and you want a signal-led tool with a public price.

## 7. Qualified: when inbound is the job

Qualified sells Piper, an AI SDR agent for inbound pipeline. Piper greets website visitors by video, voice or chat, captures and qualifies leads, books meetings and follows up by email. It integrates with Salesforce and more than 20 other GTM systems. Plans are Premier, Enterprise and Ultimate.

**Pricing (September 2026):** not public. You schedule a demo.

**How it differs from Alta:** Qualified covers Alex's job in depth. Piper works the buyers who come to you. Alta pairs inbound with outbound and growth agents.

**Pick it if** you run Salesforce, have steady website traffic and inbound conversion is your bottleneck.
