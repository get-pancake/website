## What Trigify did well

Trigify spent three years on one belief. In its own words, go-to-market teams decide better with "better signals and better context." It turned noisy social activity into lists a sales team could work.

Its help center describes the core jobs. You saved keyword searches with boolean queries and ran them across the professional network, X, Reddit, YouTube, podcasts and Substack. Profile engagement tracking showed who liked or commented on posts from people you picked, such as a competitor's founder or an industry voice. Workflows and integrations pushed the results onward.

Trigify also ran inside Clay. Its Clay integration finds prospects who engaged with posts containing your keywords, filtered by job title, company domain, industry, company size and location. That made Trigify a signal layer. It found the people, and your other tools did the rest.

## Why people are looking for Trigify alternatives

The reason is the calendar. On September 23, 2026, Trigify announced that its team is joining HubSpot and that the standalone platform is being retired. [Trigify's notice](https://www.trigify.io/) sets out what happens next:

- **Access ends October 22.** For customers on the standard notice period, accounts close at 11:59pm BST on October 22, 2026. Contracts with a different notice period get their dates confirmed directly.
- **Everything downstream stops the same night.** Integrations, API access, monitoring and signal collection end with account access.
- **Your data stays behind.** Trigify customer data will not move to HubSpot. You export it yourself with the existing export options before access ends.
- **No renewal.** Subscriptions don't renew past the service end date.

### A switching checklist before October 22

1. **Export what you built.** Saved searches, keyword lists, monitored profiles and past results. The keywords and profiles matter most, because they record who your buyers listen to.
2. **List every place Trigify writes to.** Clay tables, CRM properties, Slack alerts, webhooks and scripts on the API all go quiet on October 22.
3. **Decide whether you want a feed or a result.** A feed is signal data piped into your stack. A result is a lead you can talk to, with the conversation already started. Your answer picks the tool.
4. **Run the replacement in parallel.** Start it now, give it the same keywords and profiles, and compare the leads before the cutoff.

## 1. Pancake: the pick when the goal is conversations with buyers

Pancake is an AI GTM team for founders and small B2B companies. Its agents find the people showing buying signals and start conversations with them. If you used Trigify to decide who to talk to, Pancake makes that call every morning and then opens the conversation for you.

- **Picks up where your Trigify searches left off.** Pancake tracks six kinds of signal. Four are social: a post on a topic you choose, and engagement with a competitor, with a voice your buyers follow, or with your own posts. Those four map to Trigify's keyword searches and profile engagement tracking. The other two read job posts: open roles that match your buyer, and posts that name a tool you replace.
- **Wakes you up to new leads.** Each morning's leads name the signal behind them, so you know why a person is on the list before anyone reaches out. Approve them in the app or in Slack, where each one is posted.
- **Opens the conversation under your name.** Everything goes out from your own account on the professional network. Pancake visits the profile, likes a recent post and sends an invite without a note, then follows with up to three messages. Message one asks a single light question about the signal, with no pitch attached.
- **Starts from your website.** Pancake reads your site into its GTM Brain: positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. Every result teaches it more about what works.
- **Runs from your AI chat.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Codex through Pancake's MCP server. From that chat you can read the GTM Brain, review leads, change a signal or start outreach.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month, flat](/pricing) for every agent. There are no seats or usage fees to count. A card starts the 3-day free trial, and you can cancel anytime.

**How it differs from Trigify:** Trigify handed you a list, and another tool did the outreach. Pancake runs the whole loop each day: spot the signal, pick the lead, open the conversation. It also writes articles built to show up in Google and in AI answers, so buyers find you too. Pancake is built to act on signals itself, so if your stack needs a raw signal feed piped into Clay or a CRM, Teamfluence, Octolens or Clay below fit that job better.

**Pick it if** you are a founder or run a small B2B team, and what you wanted from Trigify was more conversations with the right people. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. Teamfluence: engagement tracking across your whole team

Teamfluence sums up its pitch in one line: "Your team's Network is your team's Sales Pipeline." It watches engagement on the professional network across every teammate's profile and your company page. That covers profile visits, likes, comments and shares on posts, new connections and company page follows. It also tracks engagement on competitor and influencer posts, plus custom keywords.

Signals feed "if this, then that" workflows. Teamfluence can enrich a lead with contact data, match it against your ideal customer profile, spread invites across team accounts and send qualified leads to HubSpot, Slack or Clay through webhooks.

**Pricing (September 2026, listed in euros):** Economy costs €89 per seat per month with 750 workflow credits, for up to 10 people. Business costs €129 per seat per month with 1,500 credits. GTM Unlimited costs €199 per workspace per month, billed monthly, with two seats and the company page included. Extra seats cost €89 each. Teamfluence states a strict no-discount policy.

**How it differs from Trigify:** Teamfluence starts from your own team's accounts. Trigify started from keywords and chosen profiles. Teamfluence is built around the professional network, where Trigify also reached X, Reddit, YouTube and podcasts.

**Pick it if** your team posts often and you want every engagement across every teammate's profile turned into leads, with workflows you control.

## 3. Octolens: keyword listening across the web

Octolens describes itself as "one API for every mention across the internet." It tracks keywords across more than 15 platforms, including X, Reddit, Hacker News, YouTube, GitHub, Stack Overflow, Bluesky, podcasts, newsletters and news sites. AI relevance scoring and sentiment labels cut the noise, and AI summaries show what came in.

Results go where you need them. Octolens offers a REST API, webhooks that post each mention, Slack routing, email alerts and an MCP server for agents such as Claude and Cursor.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Pro costs $159 a month billed annually, with 15,000 mentions and 10 keywords. Scale costs $499 a month billed annually, with 50,000 mentions and 40 keywords. Paying month to month costs more, since annual billing saves up to 20%. Mentions past your quota bill from $0.01 each. A 7-day free trial includes the Pro features.

**How it differs from Trigify:** Octolens is the nearest match for Trigify's keyword searches across many platforms, and it reaches further, into developer forums and news. It is built around mentions, meaning who wrote about your keyword, rather than who reacted to a given person's posts.

**Pick it if** your buyers talk on X, Reddit or Hacker News, and you want the mentions in your own stack through an API or MCP.

## 4. Clay: if your Trigify signals fed Clay tables

Clay is a data and workflow platform for go-to-market teams, and Trigify was one of its integrations. Clay Signals now covers much of the same ground. Its signals page lists job changes, website visits, event attendance, social mentions, technology used, product activations and support tickets. On social, Clay monitors content with your keywords on the professional network, Reddit and YouTube, and listens for brand or competitor mentions.

**Pricing (September 2026):** a free plan with 500 actions and 100 data credits a month, without signals. Launch starts at $185 a month, or $167 a month billed annually, and adds job change, company news and social listening signals. Growth starts at $495 a month, or $446 a month billed annually, and adds web intent signals. Enterprise is custom. A 14-day trial is available.

**How it differs from Trigify:** Clay keeps the signal, the enrichment and the workflow in one table, so a Trigify column becomes a native Clay signal. You still design the table and the outreach steps yourself.

**Pick it if** Clay is already your GTM workspace and you want the fewest moving parts after October 22.

## 5. Gojiberry: signals plus outreach in one agent

Gojiberry sells an AI sales agent. It reads your website to learn what you sell, finds high-intent prospects and runs outreach across email and social. Its signals include competitor engagement, follows of your company and activity in your space, plus lookalikes of your best customers. Every lead is filtered to match your ideal customer profile. Replies land in a unified inbox, and you choose full automation or approval before sending. It connects to HubSpot and Pipedrive, and to Claude and other tools through its API and MCP.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Pro costs $99 a month for two AI agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted a month. A Custom plan covers sales teams of five or more and outbound agencies. There is a free trial.

**How it differs from Trigify:** Gojiberry acts on the signals it finds, where Trigify stopped at the signal. Next to Pancake, it adds email, while Pancake adds articles for Google and AI answers. Our [Gojiberry vs Pancake comparison](/gojiberry-vs-pancake) goes deeper.

**Pick it if** you want one agent to pick leads and reach them by email as well as on social.

## 6. Common Room: every signal for a larger sales org

Common Room sells buyer intelligence that "turns intent into pipeline." It pulls signals from product usage, website visits, GitHub activity, your CRM, marketing and engagement into one view of each buyer, with social and community engagement on every plan. Its RoomieAI handles account research, contact research, message personalization and live guidance during calls. It has more than 50 integrations, including Salesforce and HubSpot, and surfaces insights in Slack, Outreach and email.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Essential costs $2,500 a month billed annually, with 5 seats, up to 100,000 contacts and unlimited alerts, workflows and segments. Advanced and Enterprise are custom. The pricing page lists no free plan.

**How it differs from Trigify:** Common Room is a full buyer intelligence platform with social as one source among many. Its entry plan is priced for a sales team.

**Pick it if** you run a sales team that wants product, web and social signals in one place, and the budget fits.
