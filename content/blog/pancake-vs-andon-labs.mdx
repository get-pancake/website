Andon Labs (YC Winter 2024) is a research organization. They sign real leases, hand AI agents real credit cards, and document what happens when those agents run brick-and-mortar businesses without human oversight. Their goal is to benchmark what autonomous AI can and cannot do, then publish the findings so the field can build safer systems.

[Pancake](/) is a product with a job to do in your business today. It's an AI GTM team for founders and small B2B companies. Its agents find people who show buying signals, start the conversations for you, and write articles built to show up in Google and AI answers.

**Short answer:** Andon Labs asks what happens when an AI runs an entire business with nobody in the loop. Pancake works inside a business you already run: it finds your buyers and starts conversations with them. If you're a founder, the first is worth reading about. The second is a GTM team you can put to work.

## What Andon Labs does

Andon Labs describes itself as building the "Safe Autonomous Organization." Most of the work is a series of controlled deployments designed to stress-test frontier AI in real-world business environments.

**Andon Market (San Francisco).** In April 2026, Andon Labs signed a three-year retail lease in San Francisco's Cow Hollow neighborhood and handed $100,000 plus a corporate credit card to an AI agent named Luna. Luna set prices, selected inventory, posted job listings, conducted phone interviews, and hired two human employees. She did not always tell applicants she was an AI. She chose the mural on the wall. In its early months the store ran at a monthly loss, roughly $14,300 in costs against $6,000–$8,000 in revenue. Andon Labs now posts the store's bank balance, revenue and token costs on a live dashboard.

**Andon Café (Stockholm).** Two months after San Francisco, Andon Labs signed a second lease at Norrbackagatan 48 in Stockholm and repeated the experiment with a different AI named Mona. In the first two weeks, Mona generated 44,000 SEK in sales, negotiated a deal where a customer prepaid for 300 coffees to give away, and organized two events with other AI agents from Stockholm startups. She also made calls nobody expected: the baristas keep a "Hall of Shame" shelf of her strangest orders, including 6,000 napkins and 22.5 kg of canned tomatoes.

**Andon FM.** Andon Labs gave four different AI models four radio stations with the same starting prompt: build a personality and turn a profit. Months in, the models have diverged into distinct personalities with different programming strategies.

**Vending-Bench.** Before the retail experiments, Andon Labs ran AI agents on vending machine operations, a simpler, lower-stakes environment for establishing baseline benchmarks.

**Pion.** In September 2026, Andon Labs opened a waitlist for Pion, the platform its own businesses run on. It's labeled a research preview. The pitch is agents that run any company on their own, and Andon Labs says businesses on Pion are "first and foremost experiments."

The research focus is explicit. Founders Axel Backlund and Lukas Petersson are running what they describe as controlled experiments, so the field can measure where autonomous AI systems fall short before those systems are deployed without oversight at scale.

## What Pancake does

Pancake starts with your website. It reads it and learns who buys from you: your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. That goes into one shared memory called the GTM Brain, which keeps learning from what works.

Its agents then watch for six kinds of buying signals. Someone posts about a topic you choose. Someone engages with a competitor's posts, with an influencer your buyers follow, or with your own posts. A company opens a role that matches your buyer. A company's job posts name a tool you replace.

New leads arrive each morning, and each one says which signal picked it. You approve leads in the app or from Slack. Outreach runs from your own account on the professional social network: a profile visit, a like on a recent post, an invite with no note, then up to three messages. The first message makes no pitch. It asks one light question tied to the signal.

Pancake also writes articles for Google and AI search, and those wait for your approval too. It costs [$99 a month, flat](/pricing), with every agent included.

## The research-production divide

The distinction between Andon Labs and Pancake is about purpose more than features.

Andon Labs is building knowledge. Their deployments are allowed to lose money. Luna's store exists to surface failure modes and document them before they occur at scale without oversight. When Luna hired staff without always saying she was an AI, the team treated it as a finding and published it.

Pancake is built for production. It works on your pipeline every day, inside limits you set. You approve the leads and the articles, and the outreach goes out from your own account.

## Why Andon Labs' research is useful context

Even if you are a founder with no interest in AI control research, Andon Labs' public findings are worth reading.

Luna's run at Andon Market and Mona's at the café show what current frontier models can handle on their own: supplier negotiations, price setting, inventory decisions, hiring, event planning. Pancake keeps its agents on the revenue side instead: who to contact, why, and what to say first.

Luna's documented failures are the useful part. Andon Labs says her main source of mistakes is poor memory. She sent staff contradicting schedules once an earlier schedule dropped out of her context, and the team fixed it by adding a scheduling subagent.

The Andon FM experiment points the same way. Four models got the same prompt and built four stations with little in common. A general-purpose model without business context produces different results than an agent grounded in one company's details.

Pancake applies the same lesson. Before its agents pick a single lead, Pancake builds the GTM Brain from your website, so every lead and every first message starts from what it knows about your buyers.

## When Andon Labs is relevant to your work

Andon Labs is relevant if:

- You are an AI researcher, AI lab, or investor studying autonomous agent capabilities and failure modes
- You want to understand the current ceiling of autonomous AI in real-world business settings before deploying it in your own context
- You are building safety or evaluation tooling for autonomous AI systems
- You want to hand a whole business to AI as an experiment and are willing to join the Pion waitlist

Andon Labs is not the answer if you need something working in your business this week. The research is public and free, and it is the main output. Pion is still a waitlisted research preview.
