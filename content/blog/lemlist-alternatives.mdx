**TL;DR:** lemlist is a strong multichannel outreach platform for teams that want to build and run their own sequences. If what you want is buyers and conversations without running a platform, pick Pancake: its AI agents watch buying signals, bring you new leads each morning and start the conversations from your own account, for $99 a month flat. Apollo is the closest all-in-one swap and has a free plan. La Growth Machine and Dripify fit sequences on the professional network plus email. Amplemarket fits funded sales teams, Gojiberry fits signal-led outreach across email and social, and Instantly fits cold email at volume.

Every price and feature below comes from the vendor's own homepage and pricing page, read on September 24, 2026.

## What lemlist does well

The product covers the whole outbound loop in one place. Its lead database lists more than 650 million leads, with an email and phone number finder built in. Sequences can mix email, steps on the professional network, calls, WhatsApp and SMS.

Deliverability is where lemlist goes deepest. lemlist ships email warm-up, a deliverability hub and inbox monitoring, which matters once a team runs many mailboxes.

The AI layer has grown too. lemAgent builds a campaign from a chat. You describe the goal and your ideal customer, and it finds the leads, builds the sequence and writes every message across channels. You review the whole campaign before launch. Intent signal agents flag hiring changes, website visits, funding rounds, job changes, engagement and tech stack changes. lemlist also offers an MCP server, a CLI and native Salesforce, HubSpot and Attio integrations.

On price, the Email plan includes unlimited users and senders, and the 14-day trial needs no card. It fits a team that wants hands-on control of every sequence. [lemlist vs Pancake](/lemlist-vs-pancake) walks through where each one comes out ahead.

## Why people look for lemlist alternatives

- **Per-user pricing once you go multichannel.** The Email plan is flat, but social steps and calls live on the Multichannel plan at $109 per user per month. A three-person team pays $327 a month on monthly billing.
- **Data and signals bill separately.** Enrichment and buying intent signals are Data plans paid in credits, from $20 and $29 a month.
- **It is a platform you run.** Someone still defines the audience, reviews what lemAgent builds and watches mailbox health. A founder without a sales team often lacks those hours.
- **Email volume.** The Email plan caps sending at 50,000 emails a month. Teams sending across dozens of mailboxes compare with email-first tools built around volume.
- **Budget.** Sequencers built around one social channel start below lemlist's Multichannel seat.

## 1. Pancake: buyers and conversations without running a platform

Where lemlist hands your team a campaign builder, Pancake hands you the conversations. It is an AI GTM team: AI agents that decide each day who is worth talking to, from live buying signals, and open the conversation from your own account.

- **Starts from your website.** Pancake reads your site and turns it into the GTM Brain, one shared memory of your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. Every result feeds back into it.
- **Picks people from six buying signals.** Four come from the feed: posts on a topic you pick, and engagement with a competitor, with a voice your buyers follow, or with your own brand. Two come from job posts: open roles that match your buyer, and listings that name a tool you replace.
- **Hands you the day's leads each morning.** Every lead names the signal that surfaced it, so you know why this person made the list. You approve them in the app or from Slack, which gets a post for every new lead.
- **Makes the first touches for you.** On the professional network your buyers use, Pancake visits the profile, likes a recent post and sends an invite with no note. Up to three messages follow. Message one makes no pitch: it asks a single question about the signal.
- **Writes, and plugs into your AI.** It drafts articles aimed at Google and AI answers, for you to approve. Through its MCP server, Claude, ChatGPT or Codex can read the GTM Brain, review leads or change signals from a chat.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month, flat](/pricing), whatever the size of your team. Every agent sits in the one plan, and nothing is metered. The 3-day free trial asks for a card.

**How it differs from lemlist:** lemlist gives your team the controls: audience, sequence steps, channels and mailboxes, with lemAgent to draft campaigns for your review. Pancake makes the daily call on who to contact and why, then starts each conversation from your account, with approvals on leads and articles. lemlist's Multichannel bill rises with every user, and signals draw on credits. Pancake's price stays put. Pancake works the professional network, not the inbox, so if your pipeline runs on cold email volume, keep lemlist's Email plan or look at Instantly.

**Pick it if** you run a founder-led or small B2B team with nobody free to operate a sales platform, and what you want each week is conversations with people who showed a buying signal. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. Apollo: data, sequences and a dialer in one login

Apollo sells a GTM platform built on its own B2B data: more than 240 million contacts and 30 million companies. Prospecting, sequences and a dialer share one workspace. Buying signals cover intent topics, job changes and website visits. Its agents research accounts, build lists and write outreach, and you can work from your CRM, Gmail, Claude or ChatGPT.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Free plan gives 900 credits per seat per year, two sequences and one intent topic. Basic costs $49 per seat per month billed annually, with unlimited sequences and a 14-day trial. Professional costs $79. Organization costs $119 per seat with a three-seat minimum. Monthly billing costs more.

**How it differs from lemlist:** Apollo leads with data. Every plan, the free one included, comes with intent topics and website visitor identification, while lemlist bills signals through credits. Apollo prices every paid plan per seat, where lemlist's Email plan covers unlimited users.

**Pick it if** you want database, sequences and dialer under one login, and a free plan to test them.

## 3. La Growth Machine: conditional sequences across social and email

La Growth Machine (LGM) builds sequences that move between the professional network and email. Pro adds calls, and Ultimate adds X. Conditions branch each sequence: if a lead accepts your invite, send a message, and if not, switch to email. It adds AI-written messages and AI voice messages, and Pro puts both channels in one shared inbox. Sending runs in the cloud, with a dedicated mobile proxy for each identity.

**Pricing (September 2026, per identity):** Basic costs $70 a month ($60 billed annually), Pro $135 ($110) and Ultimate $195 ($165). Each plan includes enriched leads every month: 250, 400 or 1,000. The 14-day trial needs no card.

**How it differs from lemlist:** you pay per identity, the profile that sends. Team members without an identity are included: three on Basic, 25 on Pro. lemlist adds WhatsApp and SMS, and its lead database is far larger.

**Pick it if** your outreach lives on the professional network and email, and you want branching sequences with a shared inbox.

## 4. Amplemarket: signals and AI drafting for sales teams

Amplemarket pairs its own data with Duo, which it calls an AI sales agent. Duo watches signals such as website visits, job changes, competitor engagement and social activity. It then drafts multichannel sequences across email, calls and social steps, and a rep approves them. A parallel dialer and deliverability tools sit in the same platform.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the entry plan, Startup, is $600 a month on an annual term, for two users and 27,000 contacts. Growth (four users) and Elite (ten users) are quoted on request. Each plan comes with a free trial.

**How it differs from lemlist:** Amplemarket sells annual contracts to sales teams. lemlist starts self-serve and month to month.

**Pick it if** you manage a funded team of reps and want signals to feed AI-drafted sequences under one annual contract.

## 5. Gojiberry: an AI agent for signal-led outbound

Gojiberry, backed by Y Combinator, starts from your website. Its agent learns your business, spots buying and social signals such as competitor engagement, and filters prospects against your ideal buyer profile. It then runs outreach across email and social, in full auto or with approval before each send. Native connections include HubSpot, Pipedrive and Claude, plus an API and MCP.

**Pricing (September 2026):** $99 a month on Pro buys two AI agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted each month. Teams of five or more sellers and outbound agencies get a custom plan. Gojiberry offers a free trial.

**How it differs from lemlist:** there are no sequences to build. Gojiberry's agents prospect around the clock and write each message. lemAgent builds a campaign when you ask, for you to review and launch. Our [Gojiberry vs Pancake](/gojiberry-vs-pancake) page puts the two side by side.

**Pick it if** you want an agent to run outbound across email and social, synced to your CRM.

## 6. Instantly: cold email at volume

Instantly is built for cold email. Every outreach plan includes unlimited email accounts and unlimited warm-up. The Light Speed plan adds dedicated server and IP pools to protect deliverability. A database of more than 450 million B2B leads, an AI sales agent and a ChatGPT connection sit alongside the sender.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Growth outreach plan costs $47 a month ($37.60 billed annually) for 5,000 emails and 1,000 uploaded contacts. Hypergrowth costs $97 and Light Speed $358. Lead credits are sold apart, and bundles with both start at $94 a month. You can start without a card.

**How it differs from lemlist:** Instantly prices by send volume and uploaded contacts, and every plan allows unlimited email accounts. It is email-first, while lemlist puts more channels in one sequence.

**Pick it if** cold email is your main channel and you run many mailboxes.

## 7. Dripify: the lowest price per seat

Dripify automates sequences that mix actions on the professional network with email steps, joined by delays and conditions. It adds an email finder, a dedicated inbox, team management and built-in account protection.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Basic costs $39 per user per month billed annually ($59 monthly), with one campaign and limited daily quotas. Pro costs $59 ($79 monthly) and adds unlimited campaigns. Advanced costs $79 ($99 monthly). Every paid plan comes with 100 email finder credits each month. The 7-day trial needs no card.

**How it differs from lemlist:** it costs less per seat than lemlist's Multichannel plan. Its plans list sequences on two channels, the professional network and email.

**Pick it if** price per seat decides it and your outreach lives on the professional network.
