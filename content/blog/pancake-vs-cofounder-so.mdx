## What happened to Cofounder.so

Cofounder.so was a chat-based AI advisor for early-stage founders. You asked it to review a pitch, research a market or pressure-test a business model, and it answered with recommendations to act on.

In September 2026, cofounder.so shows a Namecheap parking page instead of the product. We have no date or reason for the change. Don't mix it up with Cofounder.co: that is a different site, and it is live (see #2 below).

## 1. Pancake: best when the product works and you need customers

Cofounder.so told you about your market. Pancake goes into it. Built for founders and small B2B companies, this AI GTM team picks out the people showing buying signals and, once you approve them, starts talking to them from your own account.

It begins with your website. Pancake turns what it reads into the GTM Brain: your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections, held in one shared memory. Every agent draws on it, and every result makes it sharper.

Signals come in six kinds. Some are about people: who posts about a topic you choose, and who engages with posts from a competitor, from you or from a voice your buyers follow. Others are about companies: roles they are hiring for that match your buyer, and tools their job posts name.

A fresh batch of leads arrives each morning with the reason behind every pick. Approve the ones you want in the app or in Slack. Pancake then works each approved lead from your own account on the professional social network, with a profile visit, a like, an invite and up to three messages. It opens with a question about the signal rather than a pitch.

It also writes articles aimed at Google and at AI answers, and you approve each one before it goes out. Approvals sit on leads and articles, so you stay in control.

Prefer to run things from a chat window? Pancake's MCP server connects to Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. From there you can look through the GTM Brain, check the day's leads, tune your signals and kick off outreach without switching apps.

One plan covers all of it: [$99 a month, flat](/pricing). No tiers, no seat fees, no usage meter. The 3-day free trial needs a card.

**Best for:** B2B founders who know who buys from them and need more of those people in conversation.

## 2. Cofounder (cofounder.co): the live product with the similar name

Cofounder comes from The General Intelligence Company of New York. Its agents work in three areas: launching (positioning, branding, a website), growing (market research, prospect discovery, email outreach) and operating (LLC incorporation, analytics, customer support). Risky steps, like forming the company or opening a bank account, wait for your approval.

The free trial lasts 7 days (September 2026) and includes strategy and analysis tools, a design system builder and landing page hosting. Those strategy tools cover some of the same ground as Cofounder.so. At $120 a month, Pro brings multiple agents, SEO and ICP analysis, social post publishing and an inbox for outbound and email.

**Best for:** product-stage founders who want the company side handled by agents, with sign-off on the risky parts.

## 3. Fonda: a guided path from idea to launch

Fonda is a guided course of 14 steps in four phases: Discover, Validate, Launch and Scale. The steps cover market signals and competitor analysis, interview scripts and surveys, a business case, a live website, a scope for your first version and a go-to-market plan. Each day it hands you one clear action.

Fonda is made for first-time founders, including career changers and side hustlers, and asks for no business or technical background. Pricing is a single $19 monthly plan once the 7-day free trial ends, plus an Idea Pack add-on for $9.50 (September 2026).

**Best for:** founders who used Cofounder.so for direction and want a step-by-step route to launch.

---

## 4. CoFounder.im: validation and investor documents

CoFounder.im points more than 20 AI agents at your startup idea. What comes back is a set of investor-ready documents covering the market, your competitors and the business model. Next it produces a build spec, which Claude Code or Cursor can read over an MCP server. Its Startup Mode helps with finding co-founders and managing equity.

Three projects are free as of September 2026, and each new account starts with 100 credits. Paid tiers are Idea ($9.99 a month, 1,000 credits), Launch ($29.99) and Scale ($99).

**Best for:** idea-stage founders who want their market research and business plan as documents they can share.

---

## 5. AI Cofounders: six specialists with a method each

AI Cofounders (aicofounders.co) splits the work across six specialists: Product, Marketing, Tech, Sales, Operations and Finance. You can run three of them or the full six. Each applies a named method, from Lean Startup to RICE scoring to unit economics, and progress is tracked across twelve live boards.

What you get back is finished: pitch decks, financial models, a validation plan, content calendars, first outreach and a live landing page. It pushes work out to more than 20 tools such as GitHub, Mailchimp and Twitter, and every step waits for your OK. Starting is free with no card as of September 2026, and a free idea teardown comes with it.

**Best for:** founders working alone who want a framework behind every deliverable.

## Which Cofounder.so alternative fits your stage

Cofounder.so served founders early, before there was much to sell. Match the replacement to where you are now.

- Your product works and your calendar needs buyers: **Pancake**.
- The product is built and the company side is piling up: **Cofounder**.
- You are building a first version and want a method behind each step: **AI Cofounders**.
- You need a business plan, investor documents and a build spec: **CoFounder.im**.
- You are testing an idea and want a guided path: **Fonda**.

Ask yourself one question before you pick: do you need a better plan, or more conversations with buyers? If it's the second, Pancake is built for exactly that. For the wider field, see our full ranking of the [best AI co-founder tools in 2026](/blog/best-ai-cofounder-tools-2026).
