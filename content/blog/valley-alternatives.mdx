**TL;DR:** Valley is a well-built AI outbound platform for teams whose buyers already come to them: profile viewers, post engagers and company followers. Founders and small B2B teams who want buyers from beyond their own audience should pick Pancake. It finds people posting about your problem, engaging with a rival or hiring the people you sell to, brings them to you each morning and opens the conversation from your own account, for one flat $99 a month. Gojiberry is the closest like-for-like swap. Amplemarket fits sales teams that want data, email and a dialer in one place. HeyReach, Expandi and Dripify are sending engines for people who bring their own lists and copy.

Prices and features below come from each vendor's own website, checked in September 2026.

## What Valley does well

Valley calls itself an AI outbound platform. Its pitch is outreach to people who already showed up near you. It tracks who viewed your profile, who engaged with your posts, who followed your company page and who follows your competitors. You can also describe your buyer in plain English and let Valley Deep search the web for matches.

Each lead gets enriched and scored against your ideal customer profile, and poor fits are dropped before any outreach. Valley then researches the person across more than 200 sources and writes a message in your voice. It says its writing model is trained on more than 500,000 real messages.

Outreach runs as invites and messages on the professional network, plus email. Valley handles replies and follow-ups and routes positive answers to your calendar. You can approve messages by hand or let them send on autopilot.

Account safety gets real attention. Each account gets a dedicated IP and built-in safety rails. Valley reports zero suspensions across more than 1,000 accounts and promises to refund five times your subscription if it ever causes one.

Price came down in July 2026, from $395 a month to $149 billed quarterly. The Everything plan includes unlimited leads and campaigns, API access and HubSpot and Clay integrations. Valley also offers an MCP connection. For a sales team with an active audience, Valley can replace a stack of separate tools.

## Why people look for Valley alternatives

Five reasons come up.

- **Price per connected account.** The Everything plan covers one sender. The $149 rate needs quarterly billing; month-to-month it is $199. The Scale plan, for five seats or more, is priced on a call.
- **A small orbit.** If you post rarely or your audience is still small, the pool of warm signals stays small. Net-new search through Valley Deep runs on credits: $300 a month of them come with the $499 plan, and Everything users buy them separately.
- **Signals outside your audience.** Some founders want to find people talking about the problem, hiring for it or using a tool they replace, whether or not they ever saw your profile.
- **More than outbound.** Some teams want the same system that runs outreach to write content buyers find in Google and in AI answers.
- **A full sales stack, or a bare engine.** Larger teams want a contact database, email deliverability and a dialer in one platform. Agencies often want cheap sending across many accounts and already source signals elsewhere.

## 1. Pancake: buyers from beyond your own audience

Pancake is an AI GTM team built for founders and small B2B companies. Valley starts from people who already noticed you. Pancake starts from people whose activity points to the problem you solve, and opens the conversation for you.

What you get:

- **A GTM Brain built from your website.** Pancake reads your site and learns your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. Every result feeds back into it.
- **Six signals, most of them outside your orbit.** Someone posts about a topic you chose. Someone engages with a rival's post, an influencer your buyers follow, or your own post. A company opens a role that matches your buyer. A job post names a tool you replace.
- **A morning list with reasons.** New leads land each morning, each tagged with the signal that picked it. Approve them in the app or in Slack, where Pancake posts every new lead.
- **Warm first touches from your own account.** The sequence: a profile visit, a like on a recent post, an invite sent without a note, then as many as three messages. Message one makes no pitch. It asks a light question about the signal.
- **Articles for Google and AI answers.** Pancake writes articles built to show up in both, and you approve each one.
- **Your AI chat as a control panel.** Connect Claude, ChatGPT or Codex to Pancake's MCP server and do the same work from a chat: read the GTM Brain, check leads, tune signals, launch outreach.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month, flat](/pricing), every agent included. No seats to add and no search credits to top up. Try it free for 3 days; a card is required to start.

**How it differs from Valley:** Valley reads your orbit well, competitors' followers included, and sells Valley Deep credits for net-new search. Pancake adds topic posts, influencer audiences, hiring and tool stacks, all inside the flat price. Valley stays on outbound, while Pancake also covers the inbound side with articles for search. Pancake runs its outreach on the professional network; if your motion depends on email, Valley covers both channels.

**Pick it if** your audience is still growing, you sell B2B without a sales team, and you want new buyer conversations every week from one flat plan. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. Gojiberry: the closest like-for-like swap

Gojiberry sells an AI sales agent that finds high-intent leads and contacts them for you. It detects buying and social signals, such as engagement with a competitor, people following your company and activity in your space, and it adds lookalike leads. It scores each prospect against your ideal customer before reaching out. Messages go out by email and on social networks, written by AI. You can run it fully automatic or review messages before they send. A unified inbox, email enrichment across more than 15 data providers, and HubSpot, Pipedrive and Claude integrations come in the plan.

**Pricing (September 2026):** $99 a month on Pro, which runs two AI agents and contacts up to 1,800 prospects a month. A Custom plan, quoted on a call, serves outbound agencies and sales teams of five or more. A free trial is available.

**How it differs from Valley:** the shape is close. Both pick leads from signals, score them and write the messages, and both send email as well as social messages. Gojiberry prices by prospect volume, where Valley prices per connected account. For a head-to-head with our product, see [Gojiberry vs Pancake](/gojiberry-vs-pancake).

**Pick it if** you like Valley's workflow and want the same agent to run email too, at a lower monthly price.

## 3. Amplemarket: a full sales platform

Amplemarket describes itself as an AI sales copilot for sales teams. It brings its own B2B contact database, plus intent signals, job change alerts, competitor signals and social prospecting. Its sequences run across email, phone with a parallel dialer and social actions. Its Duo AI agent helps reps find and contact prospects, and Duo Voice generates voice messages.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Startup is $600 a month on an annual contract, covering 2 users and 27,000 contacts. Growth and Elite are quoted on request. Every plan comes with a free trial.

**How it differs from Valley:** Amplemarket covers every channel and brings the data. Valley starts from warm signals around your own account and focuses on social selling plus email. Amplemarket is priced and built for a team of reps who work in it all day.

**Pick it if** you run a sales team and want data, email, phone and social in one platform.

## 4. HeyReach: many senders for agencies

HeyReach automates outreach on the professional network across many sender accounts. It sends invites, messages, follow-ups and voice notes, visits profiles, and rotates sending between accounts. Workspaces keep each client's senders, leads and campaigns apart. Leads can flow in from tools such as Clay and RB2B, and native integrations with Instantly and Smartlead add email. Its MCP server lets Claude or ChatGPT build campaigns and tag replies.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Growth costs $79 per sender a month on monthly billing, less on quarterly or yearly terms. Agency costs $999 a month for 25 senders, and Unlimited $2,999 a month. Billing follows senders, not users. Every self-serve plan has a free trial.

**How it differs from Valley:** HeyReach is the sending layer. You bring the signals, the lists and the copy. Valley finds, researches and writes for you, and names HeyReach as one of the tools it replaces.

**Pick it if** you are an agency or a team running many accounts, and you already have a way to source leads and write messages.

## 5. Expandi: build your own sequences

Expandi is an outreach automation tool for sales teams, lead generation agencies and recruiters. Its Smart Sequences combine 10 different actions and 10 conditions that react to what each prospect does. One sequence can mix invites, messages and email follow-ups. Each account gets a dedicated country-based IP, automatic warm-up and daily limit ranges. An MCP connection for Claude or ChatGPT is in private beta.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Business plan costs $99 a month, or $79 a month billed annually. Agency pricing is custom for 10 or more seats. There is a 7-day free trial.

**How it differs from Valley:** Expandi gives you the sequence builder and the safety controls. Choosing who to contact and what to say stays with you. Valley decides who to reach from signals and writes each message itself.

**Pick it if** you like designing your own sequences and want fine control over sending limits.

## 6. Dripify: the lowest entry price

Dripify runs drip campaigns across the professional network and email. It includes sequence templates, an email finder with 100 credits a month, and more than 20 personalization variables, one of them an AI-written icebreaker. Built-in account protection comes on every plan. Pro adds unlimited campaigns, a dedicated inbox, webhooks and a HubSpot integration.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Basic costs $59 per user a month, or $39 billed annually. Pro costs $79, or $59 annually. Advanced costs $99, or $79 annually. Enterprise is custom. The 7-day free trial needs no credit card.

**How it differs from Valley:** Dripify automates the steps you define. It doesn't pick leads from buying signals for you. It costs less per user.

**Pick it if** budget decides, and you already know who you want to contact.
