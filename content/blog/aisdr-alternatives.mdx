## What AiSDR does well

AiSDR sells an AI SDR built on one promise: lead quality over volume. Its agent, Ami, turns a plain-English description of your buyer into a fresh list with Live AI search. It draws on public web data and a native database of more than 300 million leads. Before it writes, it reads the prospect's recent activity and posts, company news and the problems they need solved.

Ami also plans. Its AI Strategist works out, in a plain conversation, which audience to go after and what to say, then returns full campaign ideas. The sequence builder mixes email, social steps and Aircall call steps, with rules that react to what each lead does.

It handles the plumbing too. AiSDR buys and warms your mailboxes, runs bounce checks and rotates inboxes. It syncs both ways with HubSpot on every plan, and with Salesforce on Scale. It answers replies in 5 to 10 minutes, or works in co-pilot mode if you want to review first.

You can start Solo without a sales call. For a team that wants researched email and social sequences in one tool, AiSDR is a solid choice.

## Why people look for AiSDR alternatives

Five reasons come up when teams compare.

- **Cost per contact.** Solo covers 200 AI-researched contacts a month for $250. Explore covers 800 for $900. Both work out to more than $1 per contact, before anyone replies.
- **Quarterly contracts.** Explore and Scale are billed monthly but carry a quarterly contract. AiSDR's reason is deliverability: new mailboxes need 30 days or more of warmup before they send at volume. Solo is the month-to-month plan, and there is no free trial.
- **Signals on higher tiers.** Tracking post engagement, profile visits and keywords on the professional network starts on Explore. Website visitor tracking and Salesforce sync start on Scale, at $2,500 a month.
- **The weight of cold email.** Domains, mailboxes, warmup and bounce checks come with every email-led tool. AiSDR runs them for you, but some founders would rather meet buyers where they already post and comment.
- **Fewer knobs.** A founder without a sales team often wants three things: the right people each day, a warm first message, and a price that stays flat.

## 1. Pancake: signals, morning leads and warm conversations for $99

[Pancake](/) is an AI GTM team for founders and small B2B companies. AiSDR starts from a researched contact list. Pancake starts from buying signals: its AI agents pick the people showing one and open a conversation with each of them.

What you get on the one plan:

- **A GTM Brain built from your site.** Paste your URL and Pancake maps your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. That memory gets sharper with every result.
- **Six signal types, all included.** Pancake tracks posts on a topic you name. It spots people engaging with a rival, with a voice your buyers follow, or with your own posts. It flags companies hiring for roles that match your buyer, and job posts that name a tool you replace.
- **A fresh short list each morning.** New leads land daily, and every one carries the signal behind it. Approve them in the app or straight from the Slack post Pancake sends.
- **Outreach from your own account, with no inboxes to warm.** On the professional network, Pancake visits the profile, likes a recent post and sends an invite without a note, then up to three messages. The opener asks one light question about the signal and holds back the pitch.
- **Articles for Google and AI answers.** Pancake drafts articles aimed at search and at AI answers, and each one waits for your approval.
- **Ask from Claude, ChatGPT or Codex.** Pancake's MCP server plugs your AI assistant into the workspace, so you can ask which leads came in, change a signal or kick off outreach mid-conversation.

**Pricing (September 2026):** [$99 a month, flat](/pricing), with every agent on the plan. No seats, no tiers, no usage billing, and you can cancel anytime. A card starts the 3-day free trial.

**How it differs from AiSDR:** AiSDR bills by researched contact, from $250 for 200, and keeps social signals for its $900 Explore plan. Pancake puts all six signals, the morning leads, outreach and articles under one $99 price. Outreach runs from your own account on the professional network, so there are no domains to buy and no mailboxes to warm up.

**Pick it if** you sell B2B without a sales team and want each morning to start with people worth talking to, plus a warm opener for every lead you approve.

## 2. Artisan: the closest like-for-like AI BDR

Artisan's AI BDR is called Ava. Ava finds leads in a database of more than 250 million verified B2B contacts. It runs campaigns by email and social, queues calls for your reps, and handles replies and meeting booking. It can react to intent signals and identify website visitors. You can require approval before anything sends, lock the tone and calls to action, and ban phrases.

**Pricing (September 2026):** a free plan with 300 credits a month covers lead search, lists and enrichment. Intern costs $250 a month billed annually, or $280 month to month, with 10,000 credits and about 500 leads contacted a month, plus HubSpot sync. Employee costs $600 a month billed annually, or $660 monthly, with 25,000 credits and about 1,250 leads. New accounts get a 30-day trial with 10,000 credits and no card. Larger plans are scoped with sales.

**How it differs from AiSDR:** the shape is close. Both research leads, write the outreach and handle replies. Artisan bills in credits, about 20 per person contacted in an end-to-end campaign, and lets you try it free. AiSDR bills by researched contact and has no free plan or trial.

**Pick it if** you want a like-for-like AI BDR, a free way to explore first, and room to grow into a sales-led plan.

## 3. Reply.io (Jason AI SDR): an AI SDR on a mature sequencer

Reply.io has sold sales engagement software for more than 10 years. Its AI SDR, Jason, pulls contacts from a real-time B2B database, reads intent signals and personalizes each message, in autopilot or copilot mode. Jason runs on Reply's multichannel platform, with unlimited mailboxes and warmups, website visitor reveals and CSM onboarding.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Jason AI SDR Starter plan starts at $500 a month billed annually, for 1,000 active contacts. Growth starts at $1,500 for 5,000. Monthly billing costs more. If you would rather build sequences yourself, the Email Volume plan starts at $49 per user a month and Multichannel at $89, both billed annually. Multichannel comes with a 14-day free trial.

**How it differs from AiSDR:** Reply gives you a full sequencer under the AI, so you can run AI-led and hand-built campaigns side by side. Its entry AI SDR plan covers 1,000 active contacts against Solo's 200 researched ones, but the $500 price assumes a year's commitment, twice what Solo costs month to month.

**Pick it if** your team already writes its own sequences and wants an AI SDR in the same tool.

## 4. Gojiberry: signal-led outbound at $99

Gojiberry, a Y Combinator company, starts from your website to learn what you sell and who buys it. Its agents detect buying and social signals, such as engagement with competitors. They build lookalike audiences from your best customers and score every prospect. Then they reach out by email and social, and draft a reply when a prospect answers. You can run it fully autonomous or approve messages before they go out.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Pro costs $99 a month, with two AI agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted a month. It includes email enrichment across more than 15 data providers, a unified inbox, and CRM, API and MCP integrations with tools like HubSpot, Pipedrive and Claude. You can start on a free trial.

**How it differs from AiSDR:** Gojiberry is a lighter tool at a much lower price, with signals included from the first plan. AiSDR goes deeper on sequence rules, multimedia messages and CRM research.

**Pick it if** you want signal-led email and social outreach on a founder's budget. We set it against Pancake point by point in [Gojiberry vs Pancake](/gojiberry-vs-pancake).

## 5. Valley: social-first outbound from warm signals

Valley finds people who viewed your profile or your company page, engaged with your posts, or follow a competitor. It scores them against your ideal customer, researches each lead across 200+ sources and writes a message in your voice. Outreach covers email and the professional network, inside that network's safety limits, and you can approve each message or let it run on autopilot. Every plan includes reply management, Slack and email alerts, and HubSpot and Clay integrations.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Everything costs $149 a month billed quarterly, or $199 month to month. Everything + Deep costs $419 billed quarterly, or $499 monthly, and adds $300 a month of credits for live lead search across the web. Scale is custom, for five seats or more. Valley offers a 7-day free trial.

**How it differs from AiSDR:** Valley starts from warm, first-party signals on the professional network, and it costs less. AiSDR starts from a live search of the web and builds researched sequences around email.

**Pick it if** you or your team already post and get profile views, and you want to turn that attention into conversations.

## 6. Amplemarket: data, sequences and a dialer in one

Amplemarket is a full sales platform with an AI agent, Duo, on top. Duo watches signals such as job changes, website visits, social engagement and competitor reviews. It researches accounts and builds personalized multichannel sequences: email, calls and social steps, voice notes included. Around it sit Amplemarket's own contact data, a parallel dialer and deliverability tools.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the Startup plan is $600 a month on an annual term and includes two users and 27,000 contacts. Growth (four users) and Elite (ten users) are custom-priced. Growth adds Duo Voice, and Elite adds Duo Inbox for drafted replies. All plans come with a free trial.

**How it differs from AiSDR:** Amplemarket is a platform your reps work in every day, with AI helping at each step. AiSDR runs the outreach for you. Amplemarket's annual term is a longer commitment than AiSDR's quarterly one.

**Pick it if** you have a few sellers and want one product in place of a data tool, a sequencer and a dialer.

## 7. 11x: digital workers for enterprise pipelines

11x calls its agents digital workers. Alice, its outbound SDR, engages prospects across channels, handles replies and drives qualified meetings. Julian is an AI phone agent that covers the inbound side. Sequences can run across email, phone, chat, social networks, SMS and WhatsApp.

**Pricing:** not public. 11x sells through demos.

**How it differs from AiSDR:** 11x covers more channels and adds a phone agent, and it is built for larger revenue teams on sales-led contracts. AiSDR lets a small team start on a self-serve plan.

**Pick it if** you run a larger revenue team and want AI on both outbound and inbound, phone included.
