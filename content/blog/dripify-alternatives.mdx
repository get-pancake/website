## What Dripify does well

Dripify automates prospecting on the professional network. You build a sequence in a drag-and-drop builder with more than 10 actions and conditions: view a profile, like a post, follow, endorse, send an invite, send a message, and add email steps. Campaigns run on Dripify's servers, so your laptop can be off.

The safety setup is thoughtful. Each account gets a dedicated IP in your location. Actions run with randomized timing inside the working hours you set, and each plan caps daily volume.

Around the sequence, an email finder returns verified business emails, with 100 credits a month on every paid plan. Messages can pull in more than 20 variables. The Advanced plan adds A/B tests of up to three variants per step, step analytics, and team controls that stop two reps from contacting the same lead.

On price, it is one of the cheaper paid tools in the category: $39 per user per month billed annually on Basic, or $59 month to month. The 7-day trial needs no card, runs on Advanced features and doesn't charge you when it ends.

For a seller who already knows who to contact and wants those sequences to run on time, Dripify is a sound pick.

## Why people look for Dripify alternatives

- **Picking the leads is still your job.** Dripify runs sequences on people you import from searches, lists or posts. Its homepage lists high-intent signals as "coming soon".
- **Writing the sequence is your job too.** Templates and variables help, but you design the steps, write the copy and test what works.
- **Seats and quotas.** Each seat connects one account, so a team of five needs five seats. Basic allows one campaign, 20 invites and 30 messages a day.
- **More channels.** Dripify covers the network and email. Some teams want calls, X or a contact database in the same place.
- **Many senders.** Agencies that run outreach for clients compare per-sender plans, account rotation and white-label options.

## 1. Pancake: the pick when you want the leads found for you

Pancake is an AI GTM team for founders and small B2B companies. Its agents find the people showing buying signals and start conversations with them from your own account. Dripify runs the sequence once you know who to contact. Pancake answers the question that comes first: who should you talk to today, and why?

- **Learns your business.** Add your website and Pancake builds the GTM Brain: your positioning, ideal customers, offers, proof and objections. It keeps learning from what works.
- **Watches six buying signals.** Posts on a topic you choose. Engagement on a competitor's content, on your own posts, and on posts by a voice your buyers follow. On the company side, open roles that fit your buyer and job ads that mention a tool you replace.
- **Brings leads every morning.** Each lead arrives with the signal that picked it. You approve leads in the app or in Slack, where Pancake posts each new one.
- **Opens warm.** Outreach runs from your own account: a profile visit, a like on a recent post, an invite with no note, then up to three messages. The first message skips the pitch and asks one light question tied to the signal.
- **Works from Claude, ChatGPT or Codex.** Connect them through Pancake's MCP server to read the GTM Brain, review leads, adjust signals and start outreach in the same chat.
- **Writes for search too.** Pancake drafts articles built to show up in Google results and in AI answers, and each one needs your approval.

**Pricing (September 2026):** $99 a month for the whole team. Every agent is in that price, with no tiers, no seats and no usage billing. You can try it free for 3 days, with a card to start.

**How it differs from Dripify:** Dripify is a sequence engine. You bring the list, build the steps and write the templates. Pancake picks who to contact from buying signals, shows you why, and writes each first message around that signal. Dripify's price climbs with seats and plan tiers. Pancake stays at [$99 a month, flat](/pricing).

**Pick it if** you sell B2B without a sales team, and you would rather spend your week talking to buyers than building lists and sequences. [See how Pancake finds your buyers](/).

## 2. Waalaxy: the cheapest like-for-like start

Waalaxy automates invites, messages and follow-ups on the professional network. It ships pre-built sequences, syncs with CRMs such as HubSpot and Pipedrive, and includes an email finder. The Business plan adds cold email sequences, so one campaign can mix both channels.

**Pricing (September 2026):** billed yearly, Pro costs $19 per user per month with 300 invites a month. Advanced costs $39 and raises that to 800, with API access. Business costs $69 and adds cold email sequences. Quarterly and monthly billing cost more. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.

**How it differs from Dripify:** Waalaxy counts invites per month, where Dripify sets daily quotas per plan. Its entry plan costs less. Email sequences sit on the Business plan, while every Dripify plan includes them.

**Pick it if** you are new to outreach automation and want the lowest monthly bill.

## 3. Expandi: safety settings and a deep campaign builder

Expandi is a cloud-based outreach tool for sales teams, lead generation agencies and recruiters. Each account gets a dedicated country-based IP and an automatic warm-up, and you set ranges for daily actions. Its drag-and-drop builder has 19 actions and 11 conditions, with if-then logic that reacts to accepts, replies and engagement. You can add email follow-ups from any email provider. Expandi MCP, which starts outreach from a single prompt, is in beta.

**Pricing (September 2026):** one self-serve Business plan at $99 a month per seat, or $79 billed annually. The 7-day free trial doesn't charge you. Teams of more than 10 seats, and agencies that want white-label, get custom pricing from sales.

**How it differs from Dripify:** the two work alike. Expandi sells one self-serve plan, where Dripify splits features across three tiers. Its builder offers more actions and conditions, and it costs more per seat.

**Pick it if** you run complex campaigns and want warm-up and limits handled for you.

## 4. HeyReach: many senders, one inbox

HeyReach is built for agencies, sales teams and GTM experts who run outreach from several accounts. It rotates sending across connected accounts and puts every reply in one unified inbox. Sequences combine invites, messages, voice notes and profile views. Email runs through native integrations with Instantly, Smartlead and EmailBison, and HubSpot sync logs every touch. An API, webhooks, a CLI and an MCP server let you drive it from Clay, n8n, Claude or ChatGPT.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Growth costs $79 per sender per month, with 10% off quarterly and 20% off yearly. Agency costs $999 a month for 25 senders, white-label included. Unlimited costs $2,999 a month. HeyReach charges per sender, not per user, so teammates and clients join free. Growth starts with a 14-day free trial and no card. A discounted early-stage program covers companies under $250,000 ARR with fewer than five people.

**How it differs from Dripify:** Dripify ties one seat to one account. HeyReach bills by sender, and its Agency plan brings the cost per account down. Email lives in a partner tool.

**Pick it if** you run outreach for clients or across several accounts.

## 5. La Growth Machine: more channels in one sequence

La Growth Machine (LGM) runs sequences across the professional network, email, calls and X. It finds and enriches leads with verified emails, and it runs in the cloud behind dedicated mobile proxies. The Pro plan adds a unified inbox, social warming (likes, follows and profile visits), A/B tests and Slack alerts when a lead replies.

**Pricing (September 2026):** billed annually in US dollars, Basic costs $60 per identity per month and covers the network and email. Pro costs $110 and adds calls. Ultimate costs $165 and adds X plus HubSpot or Pipedrive sync. An identity is a profile that runs campaigns. LGM offers a 14-day free trial with no card.

**How it differs from Dripify:** both mix the network and email. LGM adds calls, X and lead enrichment, and costs more per identity.

**Pick it if** your sequences span several channels and you want one builder for all of them.

## 6. Gojiberry: an AI agent that finds and contacts leads

Gojiberry sells an AI agent that finds high-intent leads and contacts them. You enter your website, and it learns what you sell and who you target. It detects buying and social signals, builds lookalike audiences from your best customers and scores each prospect. Then it runs outreach by email and on social, with AI-written messages. You can run it fully automatic or approve messages before they send, and replies come back pre-written.

**Pricing (September 2026):** Pro costs $99 a month with two AI agents and up to 1,800 prospects contacted a month, plus CRM, API and MCP integrations. It starts with a free trial. Larger sales teams and agencies get custom plans.

**How it differs from Dripify:** Gojiberry picks the leads and writes the messages. Dripify runs sequences on lists you bring and copy you write.

**Pick it if** you want a signal-led agent that also sends email. It is the closest tool to Pancake on this list, and we compare the two in [Gojiberry vs Pancake](/gojiberry-vs-pancake).

## 7. Amplemarket: a full sales platform

Amplemarket is an all-in-one sales platform. Its entry plan targets startups and founder-led sales teams. It combines a B2B contact database, multichannel sequences with email, call and social steps, and AI intent signals. It also offers AI agents that research prospects and personalize outreach, plus an MCP add-on for Claude and ChatGPT. Deliverability tools and an outbound dialer round it out.

**Pricing (September 2026):** the entry Startup plan runs $600 a month, billed on an annual term, for two users and 27,000 contacts. Amplemarket quotes Growth and Elite through sales. You can try any plan free first.

**How it differs from Dripify:** Amplemarket replaces several tools at once, data and dialer included. It costs several times more and sells on annual terms.

**Pick it if** you have the budget for a yearly contract and want data, sequences, signals and calling from one vendor.
